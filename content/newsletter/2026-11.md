## Recalls to know about

- **Bodum French presses (8-cup and 12-cup, replaceable glass carafes):** Bistro, Brazil, Caffettiera, Chambord, Crema, Java, Jesper and Kenya models, sold since 1974. The glass can break during use if it's damaged or cracked, including by stirring with a metal spoon. CPSC lists 70 reports of breakage and 48 of burns. Stop using metal spoons in the carafe, don't use a cracked one, and contact Bodum for a plastic spoon and warning label. [CPSC notice](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Bodum-USA-Recalls-5-9-Million-French-Press-Coffee-Makers-with-Replaceable-Glass-Carafes-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Burn-Injury)
- **Walmart Expert Grill wire brushes:** bristles can come off and end up in food. Stop using them; Walmart offers a full refund or credit. [CPSC notice](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Walmart-Recalls-Over-4-4-Million-Expert-Grill-Brushes-Due-to-Ingestion-Hazard)
- **Whatever Works microwave grillers (K4001):** can overheat and ignite. Stop using them; full refund. [CPSC notice](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Whatever-Works-Recalls-Microwave-Grills-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-from-Fire-Hazard)

We found no new CPSC recalls for the specific models we cover.

## One useful thing

If your KitchenAid beater scrapes the bowl or misses batter, try the dime test. Put a dime in the empty stainless bowl (KitchenAid says the test is not for glass or ceramic), attach the flat beater and run the lowest speed. A small nudge on each pass means the height is right. KitchenAid says the adjustment screw needs no more than a quarter turn either way. [Full guide](https://kitchenwatcher.com/guides/kitchenaid-dime-test/)
