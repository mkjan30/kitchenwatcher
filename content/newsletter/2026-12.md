## Recalls to know about

- **Gourmia 9-Slice Digital Air Fryer Oven (model GTF7530):** the spring-loaded glass doors can close when bumped while you're reaching in for hot food. CPSC lists 57 reports and 40 burns. Sold at Target from October 2021 to June 2026. Stop using it and contact Gourmia for a free repair kit with an added doorstop. [CPSC notice](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Gourmia-Recalls-9-Slice-Digital-Air-Fryer-Ovens-Due-to-Burn-Hazard)

## One useful thing

If your Instant Pot shows "Food burn", it has sensed high heat at the bottom of the pot and turned the heat down on its own. Instant's FAQ names three usual causes: too little thin liquid, the steam release not set to sealing, or browned bits left stuck after sautéing. Turn it off, release the pressure, scrape the bottom, add thin liquid and restart. [Full guide](https://kitchenwatcher.com/guides/instant-pot-burn-message/)
