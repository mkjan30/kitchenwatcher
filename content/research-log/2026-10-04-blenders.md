## Recalls
- Ninja/SharkNinja: CPSC 16-034 (2015-11-12), BL660/BL663/BL665 Professional blenders, laceration; new warnings and instructions. BL610, BN701, DB351 not affected.
- Vitamix: 24276 (2024-06-20, expanding 18202 from 2018), Ascent and Venturist 8 oz/20 oz containers and blade bases; 13273 (2013), 64 oz Low-Profile container with blade part 103208 A, date codes 03-12 to 07-13. The 5200's standard tall container is not covered.
- nutribullet, Breville, Blendtec: no blender recalls in the API.
