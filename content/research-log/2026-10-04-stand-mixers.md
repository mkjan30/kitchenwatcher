## Recalls
No KitchenAid/Whirlpool, Cuisinart, Hamilton Beach (stand), Bosch, Kenwood (mixer) or Ankarsrum stand-mixer recall in the CPSC API. Only stand-mixer recall in the API: Sunbeam Mixmaster 90142 (1990). Hand-mixer recalls: Hamilton Beach 92043 (1992), Rival 92108 (1992), MEDport 09032 (2008). Snopes (Jan 2023) rates a viral KitchenAid "lead recall" rumor False. A rumored 2022 bowl-lift beater recall could not be verified: do not state it.

## Notes for drafting
- Amazon "Customers say" counts are Amazon's AI review summaries: attribute as such.
- Wattage: manufacturer/retailer specs only; TechGearLab shows wattage doesn't predict whipping (660 W Aucma 3.5 min vs 325 W Artisan 1 min 50 s for 1 cup cream).
- Dime test: KitchenAid producthelp confirms factory clearance, the adjustment screw (max 1/4 turn either way), reversed screw direction on some post-April-2003 bowl-lifts, and glass bowls adjusted without a dime. The "dime moves about 1/4 in per pass" figure was seen only in snippets.
- KitchenAid producthelp pages that loaded: Oil Leaking from Stand Mixer; Adjust the Beater to Bowl Clearance; Mixer Shuts Off; Beater Care; Stand Mixer Maintenance.
