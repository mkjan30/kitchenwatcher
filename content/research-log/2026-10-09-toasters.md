# Research log — toasters (2026-10-09)

Scope: 2- and 4-slice pop-up toasters only. Toaster ovens (and air-fryer toaster ovens, which overlap /best/air-fryers/) are a separate future cluster; ATK, CNET and Consumer Reports already treat them as separate categories.

Method: research.md scoring (sources 7 each, cap 35 / Amazon reviews log scale, 10k = 25 / price fit 20: $60–$250 full, under $60 = 15 per the coffee-log convention, $250–$400 linear to 0 / released in the last 24 months 10 / complaint volume 10). Trusted sources counted when they give the model an award or a "recommended" rating, not just a test slot. Recalls checked via the saferproducts.gov Recall API on 2026-10-09 (ProductName=toaster, plus RecallTitle per brand); model numbers confirmed on the cpsc.gov recall pages.
Read in full: ATK "The Best Toasters" (published 2026-04-15), TechGearLab "The Best Toasters" (updated 2025-08-14, 16 tested), Reviewed "Best Two-Slice Toasters" (updated 2025-10-22), Reviewed "Best 4-Slice Toasters" (updated 2023-09-19, dated), Popular Science "Best toasters" (updated 2024-03-29, dated).

## Fire-safety sources (readable)
- GE G9TMA2SSPSS/G9TMA4SSPSS owner's manual (Lowe's-hosted PDF): fire if covered or touching curtains/walls; operate with crumb tray in place; unplug when not in use; no oversized foods, foil or utensils.
- Breville BTA720/BTA730 instruction book (ANZ, Issue A13, assets.breville.com): crumbs in the tray are a fire hazard; jam detection turns elements off; never a knife; unplug to turn off.
- Dualit "Classic Toaster – Be Aware" support page: never leave unattended; switch off at the socket when not in use; not under cupboards when in use; no jam-filled or sugar-coated items; brush, no liquids inside slots.
- USFA cooking fire safety page (reviewed 2026-09-28): unattended equipment is the leading factor (37%) in nonconfined cooking fires; no toaster-specific advice. NFPA cooking pages not readable.
- Conflict for the cleaning guide: Breville says turn the toaster upside down and shake; GE says do not hold it upside down or shake it.
