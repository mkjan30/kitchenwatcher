# Research log — pressure cookers / multicookers (2026-11-13)

Scope: countertop electric pressure cookers and multicookers (Instant Pot, Ninja Foodi, Breville Fast Slow, Cosori, Chef iQ and similar). Stovetop pressure cookers are out of scope for this batch: only CNN Underscored puts one (T-fal Clipso) in the same roundup, and ATK, Wirecutter and Consumer Reports review them separately. Pressure cooker + air fryer combos (Ninja Foodi, Instant Pot Duo Crisp) are in scope but overlap /best/air-fryers/. Rice cookers are noted at the end as a possible later hub; no products.json entries for them.

Method: research.md scoring (sources 7 each, cap 35 / Amazon reviews log scale, 10k = 25 / price fit 20: $60–$250 full, under $60 = 15, $250–$400 linear to 0 / released in the last 24 months 10 / complaint volume 10). Trusted sources counted when they give the model an award or a "recommended"/best-of slot, not just a test slot. Recalls checked via the saferproducts.gov Recall API on 2026-11-13 (ProductName = pressure cooker, pressure, multicooker, multi-cooker, Instant Pot, Instant, Fast Slow, rice cooker, rice; RecallTitle per brand: Instant Brands, Instant, Instant Pot, SharkNinja, Ninja, Breville, Crock-Pot, Sunbeam, Cosori, CHEF iQ, Insignia, Tristar, Power Pressure, Presto, Mirro, Fagor, Zavor, Cuisinart pressure, Mueller, Gourmia, Zojirushi, Cuckoo, Aroma). cpsc.gov recall pages return 403 to scripts, so recall details below are from the API records (same text as the CPSC pages; URLs listed for the human check).

## Recalls
None of the six kept models is covered by a CPSC recall in the API.
- SharkNinja 25-247 (2025-05-01): Ninja Foodi OP300 Series Multi-Function Pressure Cookers, models OP300, OP301, OP301A, OP302, OP302BRN, OP302HCN, OP302HAQ, OP302HW, OP302HB, OP305, OP305CO, OP350CO (6.5-qt, black), plus OP300-series replacement pressure lids. About 1,846,400 (plus 184,240 in Canada). The pressure-cooking lid can be opened during use. 106 burn reports, more than 50 second- or third-degree, 26 lawsuits. Remedy: stop using the pressure function, free replacement lid; air frying and other functions can still be used. Sold at Walmart, Costco, Sam's Club, Amazon, Target and ninjakitchen.com. https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2025/SharkNinja-Recalls-1-8-Million-Foodi-Multi-Function-Pressure-Cookers-Due-to-Burn-Hazard-Serious-Burn-Injuries-Reported
- Tempo USA / ALDI 26-007 (2025-10-09): Ambiano electric pressure cookers, models 93550 (KY-318B), 96775 (KY-318A), 98527 (6L-PC), 6- and 8-qt. About 46,660. Lid can be opened before pressure is released. 11 incidents, 8 severe burns. Refund (return to ALDI or cut-cord photo to Tempo).
- Best Buy 24-013 (2023-10-26): Insignia multi-function pressure cookers NS-MC60SS8, NS-MC60SS9, NS-MC80SS9 and replacement pots NS-MCRP6NS9, NS-MCRP6SS9. About 930,000. Incorrect volume markings on the inner pot lead to overfilling; contents ejected on quick release. 31 incidents, 17 burns. Free replacement inner pot and floating locking valve. Sold Oct 2017–Jun 2023, $50–$120. Its description repeats the general rules: no more than two-thirds full, lid fully locked, float valve dropped before opening.
- Sensio 23-256 (2023-08-10): Bella, Bella Pro Series, Crux and Cooks electric (6/8/10-qt) and Bella stovetop (5/8/12-qt) pressure cookers. About 860,000. Lid can unlock and be removed during use. 63 incidents, 61 burns. Refund. Sold Sept 2015–Sept 2020.
- Sunbeam / Crock-Pot 21-035 (2020-11-24): Crock-Pot 6-Quart Express Crock Multi-Cooker SCCPPC600-V1, date codes K196JN–K365JN and L001JN–L273JN (made Jul 2017–Oct 2018). About 914,430 (plus 28,330 Canada). Can pressurize when the lid is not fully locked; lid can detach. 119 reports, 99 burns. Free replacement lid; slow cook and sauté may still be used.
- Double Insight (Instant Pot) 18-110 (2018-03-01): Instant Pot Gem 65 8-in-1 multicookers (batch codes 1728, 1730, 1731, 1734, 1746), sold only at Walmart. About 104,000. Can overheat and melt underneath (fire hazard). 107 reports, no injuries. Free replacement. Not a pressure cooker.
- Double Insight (Instant Pot) 15-741 (2015-07-15): Instant Pot "Smart" and "Smart-60" Bluetooth pressure cookers, serials 1410–1503 (made Dec 2014–Jun 2015). About 1,000 (plus 140 Canada). Thermal probe can conduct electricity; 3 shocks. Free replacement base.
- Breville 16-066 (2015-12-22): Breville Fast Slow Cooker BPR600XL, batch 1235–1529, 6-qt. About 35,600. Sealing gasket can be inserted upside down, allowing sudden pressure release; 5 burns. Free replacement gasket and updated instructions. Predecessor of the kept BPR700; not the same model.
- Older electric/stovetop recalls in the API: Rena Ware Nutrex 2153 (18-752, 2018, ~700, steam discharge at low pressure), Manttra Smart Series 38270 (08-147, 2007), Bella Cucina Zip Cookers (07-528), Ultrex 11453 (07-509), Welbilt PC501 (05-579 at HSN and 06-557 at QVC), Alcan Rochedo (83-069).
- No pressure-cooker recall in the API for Instant Pot Duo/Rio/Pro lines, Cosori, CHEF iQ, Presto, Zavor, Cuisinart, Mueller, Gourmia, Fagor, Mirro, Tristar (only a 2004 pressure-washer recall) or the "Power Pressure Cooker XL" name.
Pattern for the safety guide: of the seven pressure-cooker recalls since 2015 with a burn hazard (26-007, 25-247, 24-013, 23-256, 21-035, 18-752, 16-066), four involve a lid that can open or detach under pressure, one overfill markings, two unintended steam release (gasket upside down; discharge at low pressure).

## Primary sources (readable)
- Instant Pot FAQ, https://instantpot.com/pages/frequently-asked-questions: "food BRN Error" causes (not enough liquid, valve not sealed, deglaze after sauté) with minimum thin liquid by size: 3 qt 1 cup, 6 qt 1½ cups, 8 qt 2 cups, 10 qt 2½ cups; thicker sauces need an extra cup. Steam-leak checklist (float valve and silicone cap, sealing ring seated behind the rack, inner pot rim flush, lid moisture, handle seated, vent button up). Lid hard to close/open (float valve up = still pressurized, never force). NR for high-liquid or starchy foods (oatmeal, beans, porridge, soups); no QR for fatty, oily, thick or starchy foods or foods that expand. Replace sealing ring every 12–18 months; extra ring for sweet vs savory. PC MAX 2/3 line; 1/2 line for rice, beans, pasta, grains. "Quart size" is total volume, not cooking capacity. Pressurizing takes 10 to 40 minutes or longer. C6/C6L/C6H after spills: dry 72 hours; oil spilled on the element means stop using it. Lid and accessories top-rack dishwasher safe. Inner pot "rainbowing" is normal.
- Instant Pot Rio Plus Wide user manual (QVC-hosted PDF, https://www.qvc.com/footers/cd/pdf/K89432_Manual.pdf): test run with 3 cups water, Pressure Cook 5 min, Keep Warm off, vent when done; NR vs QR steps (QR for vegetables and seafood); minimum liquid 2 cups for 7.5 qt; error code table: C1/C2/C6/C6H/C6L faulty sensor → Consumer Care; C5 no pot or no water; C7 or NoPr = heating element failed, not enough liquid, or quick-release in Vent; Lid = wrong lid position for program; Food burn = high temperature at pot bottom, cooker lowers heat; PrSE = pressure built in a non-pressure program. Troubleshooting rows for steam leaking from the side of the lid (missing/damaged/dirty ring, rack warped, pot rim misshapen); minor hissing during cooking is normal. "DO NOT use this appliance for deep frying or pressure frying with oil." Sealing ring part 211-1004-01.
- Instant Pot Rio Wide user manual (Canadian Tire-hosted PDF, https://media-www.canadiantire.ca/manual/product/0439809/0439809-1-76932d79-d779-4ab7-9028-c556b46ae86c.pdf): float valve drops into the lid when all steam is released; replace sealing ring every 12–18 months. Conflict: its test run says "3 quarts water" where the Rio Plus manual says 3 cups. Flag on the water-test page; do not resolve it ourselves.
- Instant Pot cooking time tables, https://instantpot.com/pages/cooking-time-tables: multicooker rice times white, jasmine and basmati 4 min, brown 20 min, wild 20 min (manufacturer).
- NCHFP: https://nchfp.uga.edu/publications/nchfp/factsheets/electric_cookers.html (resolves to https://nchfp.uga.edu/newsflash/canning-in-electric-multi-cookers) and https://nchfp.uga.edu/blog/can-i-can-in-a-multi-cooker: USDA canning processes are not supported in electric multi-cookers even with a "canning" button; processes were developed for stovetop canners holding four or more quart jars; temperature, not pressure, is what matters; come-up and cool-down heat count toward the process; risk for low-acid foods is botulism. Exception: Ball automatic canner (acid foods only), a dedicated appliance.

## Notes for drafting
- Every recommender agrees most electric cookers pressure-cook well; differences are usability, pot material and slow cooking. Slow cooking is the recurring failure: ATK found the Pro 8, Pro 6, Rio, Duo 6 and Duo 8 weak at it; Reviewed's Duo left onions uncooked after 12 hours. Cosori (ATK) and Breville (CR) are the exceptions; CR also rates the Duo Crisp 8-qt well at slow cooking. Say so on the hub.
- Time to pressure (Wirecutter, a pot of beans): Pro 14:24, Rio 14:35, Duo 14:48, Duo Plus 16:08, Rio Wide Plus 11:46. Natural release can take up to 30 minutes (Wirecutter); QuickCool Tray cut up to 15 min (Wirecutter). Instant's own range is 10–40+ minutes to pressurize. Good table material; credit Wirecutter.
- Sauté levels: Rio 3, Pro 5 (Wirecutter). Pro pot oven-safe to 450 °F (Wirecutter). Cooking surface diameters: Pro 8 8.25 in, Pro 6 7.75 in, Chef iQ 8 in, Cosori 7 in, Cuisinart CPC-900 7.5 in (ATK measurements).
- Sizes conflict: ATK says 8 qt is best for most cooks; Wirecutter, Good Housekeeping (2022) and CNET favor 6 qt. CNET relays Instant's guidance: 3 qt for one or two, 6 qt for families up to six, 8 qt for large households.
- TechGearLab overall scores: Duo Plus V4 86, Breville 84, Ninja Foodi Smart XL 83, Ninja Foodi Deluxe XL 80, Presto 02141 75. The per-metric numbers in the page source are unlabeled in the scrape; use only the overall scores unless a human confirms the metric mapping.
- Burn message: ATK got a C7 on the Duo Crisp with Ultimate Lid during beef stew and says the manual did not explain it; CR got "Food Burn" every time it pressure-cooked dried-bean chili in the Instant Pot Max. ATK also got "NO PRESSURE" on the Ninja Foodi OL601. Good attributed examples for the error-code and burn guides.
- Sealing ring odor: Wirecutter bakes the cleaned ring at 250 °F for 20–30 min and suggests buying parts from Instant, not Amazon, to avoid counterfeits; CNN suggests a baking-soda soak. These are tester methods, not Instant's instructions; attribute them. Instant says replace every 12–18 months.
- Instant Brands filed for bankruptcy in June 2023 and kept operating (Wirecutter). instanthome.com no longer loads for us; link instantpot.com.
- CNET's "2026" Instant Pot page was last updated Feb 2025; Popular Science (2023) and Good Housekeeping via Yahoo (2022) are dated. Use them only as secondary support.
- Recipes remain paused. The rice page is a how-to (ratios, times, fill lines, sources), not a recipe with a Recipe card.
