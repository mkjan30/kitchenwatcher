## Notes for drafting
- Wattage: three numbers get mixed up. Input (Toshiba 1,550 W, Breville 1,350 W, GÅTEBO 1,350 W) vs output (1,100 / 900 / 900 W). Tom's spec box printed input as "Power". GÅTEBO's manual says 900 W in the spec table and "rated 1000 watts by using the IEC Test Procedure" in the text. ATK: 700–1,250 W in its test; higher isn't necessarily better; ~900–1,000 W lets you follow package times; with high-power ovens use less time or lower power; the 700 W Galanz needed more time but never burned food. Tom's and Food Network: 800–1,200 W "sufficient". RTINGS: scores mostly come down to power; buy at least 1,000 W output (staff comment, Nov 2024).
- Size: ATK found 0.9–1.0 cu ft adequate and even its smallest turntable (9⅝ in) fit a dinner plate; CR says manufacturers' capacity often includes space you can't use; RTINGS measured EM131A5C-BS at 1.06 cu ft (rated 1.2), SMC2266KS at 2.08 (rated 2.2), Breville Smooth Wave 1.10 (ATK lists 1.2). Tom's and Food Network: measure the turntable and your largest dish; Food Network: 0.8–1.2 cu ft for most people.
- Inverter: sources disagree. ATK says inverter and traditional are "virtually indistinguishable" at full power, differ at low power, but the inverter Panasonic NN-SN67KS gave no measurable benefit and burned coconut. RTINGS says no real-world benefit except tiny tasks. Reviewed says inverters "perform better" and its inverter picks defrost gently. Say so.
- Evenness: RTINGS says all microwaves heat evenly ("the physics"); Reviewed and ATK report uneven heating on specific models (GE GCST09N1WSS hot spots; Toshiba ML-EM45P "not the most even"). CR says its tests "found big differences in overall performance". Sources disagree; attribute each.
- Foil: Toshiba manual permits small smooth shielding pieces 1 in from walls; GÅTEBO manual describes how foil arcs and forbids covering racks; Breville says no foil; FDA says "generally" no metal or foil. The foil guide should say it depends on the manual.
- Cleaning: lemon-water steam methods differ by manual (Breville 1 cup + juice of a lemon, 5 min at 100%; IKEA half a lemon in 300 ml, 3 min).
