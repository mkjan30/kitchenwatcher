I'm Maksud, and I run Kitchen Watcher. I started it because choosing a kitchen appliance usually means a dozen open tabs: spec sheets, lab tests, owner complaints and prices that change every week. Here I pull that evidence into one page, say exactly where every number comes from, and keep track of prices so you can tell a real deal from a fake markdown. I don't test products hands-on; I read the tests, reports and recalls so you don't have to, and I tell you when the evidence is thin.
