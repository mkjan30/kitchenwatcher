## How I test

Each model gets the same protocol, detailed on [how we test](/how-we-test/): cold-start preheat to 400°F timed with a probe at basket center, temperature logged for 10 minutes to see how far the basket drifts from the set point, noise at 1 meter, a pound of fresh-cut fries, wings, watt draw at the wall, exterior temperature, and a timed cleanup. Manufacturer specs below are labeled as specs. Everything in the measured columns comes from my own runs.
