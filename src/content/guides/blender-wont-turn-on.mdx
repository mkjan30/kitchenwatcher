When a blender won't turn on, the manuals point to three causes. The outlet has no power (a tripped GFCI or breaker), the jar or lid isn't seated so the safety interlock blocks the motor, or the thermal cutoff tripped from overload and hasn't reset yet. Between them, the Vitamix 5200 manual, Ninja's BL610 and BL621 owner's guides and nutribullet's NBF50500 user guide cover these checks, and they give different reset times.

## Is it dead from the start or did it stop mid-blend?

The two symptoms have different causes, so start here.

| Symptom | Likeliest causes (per the manuals) | Go to |
|---|---|---|
| Dead from the start: no lights, no hum | No power at the outlet, plug not fully in, jar or lid not locked | Check 1, then Check 2 |
| Ran, then the blender motor shuts off mid-blend and won't restart | Overload or thermal protection tripped | Check 3 |

## Check 1: is the outlet or cord the problem?

1. **Test the outlet.** Plug in a lamp or phone charger. Ninja's BL621 chart says to check that the plug is fully inserted and to check the fuse or circuit breaker.
2. **Reset a tripped GFCI.** In an [accepted iFixit answer](https://www.ifixit.com/Answers/View/259818/Why+won't+my+blender+turn+on) about an Oster personal blender, the responder points out that many kitchen outlets are GFCIs that can trip and cut power.
3. **Plug straight into the wall.** Ninja's [BL621 owner's guide](https://pdf.lowes.com/productdocuments/eb63db43-939c-4716-9586-a2e420802fe2/16556521.pdf) and its [BL610 guide](https://archive.org/stream/manualzilla-id-7002601/7002601_djvu.txt) both say extension cords are not recommended. Vitamix's [5200 manual](https://www.vitamix.com/media/catalog/product/file/Vitamix-5200-Owners-Manual.pdf) says to use a grounded outlet only and not to use an adapter or an extension cord.
4. **Don't force a polarized plug.** The [NBF50500 user guide](https://nbmedia.imgix.net/NBF50500_BlenderCombo_UserGuide_English_LoRes_PROOF_F20221010.pdf?dl=) says that if the plug doesn't fit, reverse it, and if it still won't fit, contact a qualified electrician. Don't modify the plug or base.
5. **Stop if the cord is damaged or the blender was dropped.** The Vitamix and Ninja manuals say not to run the appliance with a damaged cord or plug, or after it malfunctions or is dropped. The NBF50500 guide says not to use it with a damaged cord and to stop using it if it malfunctions.

Don't reach for a multimeter or open the base. The Vitamix 5200 manual says any repair, servicing or part replacement must be done by Vitamix or an authorized service representative.

## Check 2: is the jar or lid blocking the safety interlock?

Ninja's BL610 guide says the power button "will flash and the motor will not work if the pitcher and lid are not installed correctly." The BL621 guide, for a sibling Professional model, adds that the triangles on the lid and the pitcher handle "MUST be aligned for the blender to turn on."

Vitamix's [owner FAQ](https://www.vitamix.com/us/en_us/owners-resources/product-support/faqs) says Ascent machines show a symbol when no container is seated and won't start until one is. [iFixit's blender troubleshooting page](https://www.ifixit.com/Troubleshooting/Blender/Not+Working/479856) describes the same idea: "activator" switches in the base that the cup presses as it locks, where fluid and grime can build up and keep them from moving.

What the manuals support:

- Unplug and wipe the base collar dry, since iFixit says grime there can jam the switches. Then re-seat the jar and lock the lid with the arrows lined up, as Ninja's guides describe.
- Never press the switches with a tool or tape them down. Ninja's BL621 guide says "DO NOT try to defeat the interlock mechanism." iFixit suggests testing the switches with a screwdriver, but that conflicts with Ninja's warning, so skip it.

For Ninja light patterns, see [Ninja blender blinking lights](/guides/ninja-blender-blinking-lights/). To clean the collar and the jar, see [how to clean a blender](/guides/how-to-clean-a-blender/).

## Check 3: blender overload protection reset, by brand

If the blender ran and then cut out, its overload protection most likely tripped. Ninja's guides call it a thermal switch and nutribullet's a thermal breaker: it shuts the motor off when it overheats. Every manual below says to let it cool before restarting, except Vitamix's instructions for 220/240 V machines, which say to press the reset button.

| Model (manual) | What trips it (manufacturer) | Reset steps (manufacturer) | Wait time (manufacturer spec) | Reset button? | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

To speed it up: unplug, remove the container, blow a hair dryer on cool into the center of the base bottom | Up to 45 min, longer in a hot room (manual); the majority up to 30 min (FAQ) | No | [5200 manual](https://www.vitamix.com/media/catalog/product/file/Vitamix-5200-Owners-Manual.pdf), [Vitamix FAQ](https://www.vitamix.com/us/en_us/owners-resources/product-support/faqs) |
| Vitamix, 220/240 V models | Motor overheating | Press the black reset button on the bottom of the machine | Not published | Yes, black, on the bottom | [5200 manual](https://www.vitamix.com/media/catalog/product/file/Vitamix-5200-Owners-Manual.pdf) |
| Ninja BL610 | Appliance overheats; a thermal switch shuts off the motor | Unplug and let it cool | About 15 min | None described | [BL610 guide (archived copy)](https://archive.org/stream/manualzilla-id-7002601/7002601_djvu.txt) |
| Ninja BL621 (sibling Professional model) | Overload; "the motor will stop" | Unplug; remove and empty the container; clear any ingredients jamming the blades; cool; re-install; stay under max capacity | About 15 min | None described | [BL621 guide](https://pdf.lowes.com/productdocuments/eb63db43-939c-4716-9586-a2e420802fe2/16556521.pdf) |

Vitamix's two documents disagree. The 5200 manual says to switch off "for up to 45 minutes," while the FAQ says "the majority of shut-downs will take up to 30 minutes" and some reset in a few minutes. The BL610 guide used here is an archived copy of the older Euro-Pro-era guide, so current units may differ.

I haven't been able to open the owner's guides for the Ninja Professional Plus BN701 or the BlendBOSS DB351, so they aren't in the table.

### Vitamix stopped working mid-blend

The Vitamix 5200 manual names the usual cause. Blending "too long at too low a speed setting will overheat the machine" and trip Automatic Overload Protection. Running on High also "maximizes the motor's cooling fan." When the protection engages, the motor shuts off and may "possibly emit a light odor," per the manual.

If food stops circulating on High, the manual says the jar may be overloaded or holding an air bubble. Remove the lid plug, keep the lid in place, and use the tamper through the opening. The manual's prevention tips are in the next section.

In [TechGearLab's Vitamix 5200 review](https://www.techgearlab.com/reviews/kitchen/blender/vitamix-5200), the reviewers report the motor got warm enough during nut butter that they gave it a rest. The manual separately warns not to process nut butters for more than one minute after the mixture starts to circulate. With hot liquids, it says never to start above speed 1. More on that in [can you put hot liquid in a blender](/guides/can-you-put-hot-liquid-in-a-blender/).

## How do you keep the motor from shutting off again?

- **nutribullet NBF50500:** if you need to blend longer than a minute, blend in 1-minute intervals, wait at least a minute between them, run no more than 3 in a row, then let the contents settle 2 to 3 minutes. Use ingredients at 70°F or cooler, and always add liquid. The guide says the blender isn't meant to be used as an ice crusher.
- **Ninja BL610 and BL621:** don't overfill past the Max lines and never run the blender empty. The BL621 guide says not to blend hot liquids.
- **Vitamix 5200:** use High as much as the recipe allows, keep thick mixtures moving with the tamper, and don't run recipes slower, faster or longer than recommended.

## When to stop and call the manufacturer

Stop troubleshooting and contact the maker if:

- The blender is still dead after the full reset time, on an outlet you know works, with the jar and lid locked.
- The cord or plug is damaged, or the base was dropped. All three brands' manuals say not to use it with a damaged cord; Vitamix and Ninja also say not to use it after it has been dropped.
- It smells like more than the "light odor" the Vitamix manual describes. See [blender smells like burning](/guides/blender-smells-like-burning/).

DIY repair can cost you the warranty. The Vitamix 5200 carries a 7-year full machine warranty, and the manual says to contact Vitamix Customer Service (1-800-848-2649) before returning the product for any reason. Ninja's BL610 and BL621 guides give a one-year limited warranty that doesn't cover tampered units or repairs by a repair person not authorized by the maker (EURO-PRO in the older BL610 guide, SharkNinja in the BL621 guide). Ninja support: 1-877-646-5288.

The NBF50500 guide says to stop using a malfunctioning blender and call nutribullet customer service at 1-800-523-5993. Its warranty is a one-year limited warranty, and the guide says modifying the plug or motor base voids it.

Before you send anything back, check the [blender recall list](/guides/blender-recall-check/).
