Breville Bambino Plus flashing lights each mean one thing, per Breville's BES500 instruction book. 1 CUP and 2 CUP flashing alternately means a cleaning cycle is due after 200 extractions. 1 CUP + STEAM alternating with 2 CUP for 15 seconds means descale. MILK TEMP and MILK FOAM fully lit and flashing means a blocked steam wand. All three main buttons dark means low water.

The Bambino Plus is the BES500; Sage's UK manual covers it as BES500/SES500. Breville's manual describes no screen and no error codes, only button lights. Per the manual, you turn it on by pressing 1 CUP, 2 CUP or STEAM, and off by pressing 1 CUP and STEAM together. So the light pattern is the diagnosis.

## 1 CUP and 2 CUP flashing: alternating or together?

The search "bambino plus 1 cup 2 cup lights flashing" covers two different states. If the two buttons flash together right after you held them for 2 seconds, the manual says that is shot-volume programming, not a fault. If they take turns on their own, that is the cleaning alert.

The descale alert is easier to miss. Per the manual, it runs for only 15 seconds and pressing any of the flashing buttons cancels it. [Coffee Fix Hub's guide](https://coffeefixhub.com/guides/breville-bambino-plus-bes500-error-lights-fix) advises watching the pattern for the full 15 seconds before pressing anything, which follows from the manual: press too early and you dismiss the descale alert without seeing it.

## 1 CUP and 2 CUP lights flashing alternately: run the cleaning cycle

Per the manual, this is a flushing cycle for the group head and is separate from descaling. The steps from Breville's US manual:

1. Put the cleaning disc in the filter basket and a cleaning tablet on top. The US manual lists dual-wall baskets only; the ANZ and Sage editions also list single-wall ones. [Pressurized vs non-pressurized baskets](/guides/pressurized-vs-non-pressurized-basket/) explains the difference.
2. Lock the portafilter into the group head.
3. Empty the drip tray.
4. Put the drip tray back and place a 2 L (68 fl oz) container on it, under the portafilter and steam wand.
5. Fill the water tank to MAX.
6. Hold 1 CUP and 2 CUP for 5 seconds. Both stay lit.
7. Press either button to start. The lights alternate until the cycle finishes.

To skip it for now, press STEAM; per the manual, the alert returns at the next switch-on.

The 2023 ANZ booklet adds a note the US and Sage editions don't have: you can enter cleaning mode before the alert appears by holding 2 CUP and STEAM for 5 seconds with the machine off (all lights off).

Step 6 of the same procedure uses 1 CUP and 2 CUP, and the booklet doesn't explain the difference. Step 6 is the combination all three editions share.

## Bambino Plus descale light: what it looks like

Per Breville's manual, the descale alert is 1 CUP and STEAM flashing alternately with 2 CUP for 15 seconds, and pressing any of them dismisses it. All three editions say hard-water scale reduces brewing flow, brewing temperature, the machine's power and the taste of the espresso.

None of the three editions gives a descale interval, so I won't invent one. The Sage manual adds that replacing the supplied water filter after 3 months or 40 L (manufacturer guidance) reduces the need to descale; the US and ANZ editions don't give that figure. [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/reviews/breville-sage-bambino-plus) notes Breville also prints descaling instructions inside the water reservoir.

## How to enter Bambino Plus descale mode

Per Breville's BES500 manual:

1. Empty the drip tray.
2. Fill the tank to the DESCALE line and add descaler. The 2023 ANZ edition says to remove the water filter from the tank first; the 2018 US and Sage editions don't mention it.
3. Put a 2 L container under the portafilter and steam wand.
4. Turn the machine off (1 CUP and STEAM together) and let it cool completely.
5. With it off, hold 1 CUP and STEAM for 5 seconds. Both light up steady.
6. Press 1 CUP or STEAM to start. The two buttons alternate throughout.
7. When the solution runs out, the cycle pauses. Refill to MAX and empty the container; 1 CUP and STEAM light up when the machine is ready, and you press either to resume. The manual lists this pause as a normal step.
8. When it finishes, 1 CUP and STEAM alternate, then the machine goes to standby.

The manual warns never to remove or empty the tank mid-descale. For which descaler to use and why it matters, see [how to descale an espresso machine](/guides/how-to-descale-an-espresso-machine/).

## Milk temp and milk foam lights flashing: lower or unblock the steam wand

The manual describes two different patterns on these two lights:

- **Lights stepping downward:** the manual calls this the lower steam wand alert; the wand was left raised. Lower it and it purges automatically.
- **Lights fully on and flashing, STEAM off:** the wand is blocked and the machine stops. Hold STEAM for 5 seconds with the wand lowered (manual purge). If it stays blocked, unscrew the steam tip and clear its holes with the cleaning tool stored under the water tank.

Breville's wording is inconsistent. Both the US and ANZ editions say in one passage that the lights flash until "a manual steam purge" is done and in another until "a steam tip clean cycle" is complete. The cleaning section gives the order: purge first, then clean the tip if it stays blocked.

[Coffee Blog UK's unblocking guide](https://coffeeblog.co.uk/unblockbambinowand/) describes the same tool-under-the-tank fix, but its purge step lifts the wand, while Breville's manual purge is with the wand lowered; follow the manual.

To prevent it, the manual says to always lower the wand after steaming so it auto-purges. [TechRadar's review](https://www.techradar.com/reviews/breville-bambino-plus) describes the same purge, which pumps hot water into the drip tray, so empty the tray often.

## Why are no buttons lit? Low water or the thermal cut-out

Per the manual, low water turns off 1 CUP, 2 CUP and STEAM. Refill the tank, push it flush against the back, and the buttons light again once there is enough water.

A separate case: if the machine is on but stops working, the manual's troubleshooting table says the safety thermal cut-out may have activated because the pump overheated. Turn it off, unplug it, let it cool for about 30 to 60 minutes, then call Breville support if it persists.

## All lights flashing, or a pattern the manual doesn't list

Breville's manual does not document an all-lights-flashing pattern for the Bambino Plus. [My Morning Espresso](https://www.mymorningespresso.com/breville-bambino-barista-flashing-lights/) says all lights flashing six times in 3 seconds means an empty tank, but it lists that under the original Bambino, not the Plus, whose manual describes low water as the buttons going dark.


The safe steps below are borrowed from the manual's troubleshooting table, which doesn't cover this pattern:

1. Turn it off and unplug it.
2. Let it cool for 30 to 60 minutes.
3. Check the tank is filled and pushed fully into place, and the drip tray is pushed in.
4. If the pattern returns, contact Breville support; the machine may need service.

## Resetting the Bambino Plus: what it does and doesn't do

The manual documents only a factory espresso volume reset:

1. Turn the machine off (this section says hold 1 CUP and STEAM together for 5 seconds).
2. Hold 1 CUP and 2 CUP for 5 seconds.
3. Press 1 CUP or 2 CUP; a light flashes 3 times.

This restores the shot volumes only, about 30 ml and 60 ml per the manual. Breville never says it clears cleaning or descale alerts, so don't use it to dismiss one.

This page covers only the BES500. The original Bambino (BES450) uses different alerts; My Morning Espresso, for one, describes a flashing 2 CUP button for its cleaning and descale alert. [Bambino vs Bambino Plus](/vs/breville-bambino-vs-breville-bambino-plus/) covers how the two machines differ.
