To cook brown rice in a rice cooker, press the BROWN setting and fill to the pan's BROWN water line, measuring the rice with the cooker's own cup. Cuckoo calls the setting Mixed/Brown; Hamilton Beach calls it Whole Grain. Without a brown setting, Zojirushi says use 1.5 cups of water per cup of rice, soak it 30 minutes, and fill no more than two-thirds of capacity.

Expect anything from about 40 minutes on Zojirushi's basic NHS cooker to 84 to 107 minutes on the NS-ZCC10, per their manuals. Every number below comes from a manual or a named tester; nothing here is first-hand ([how we research](/how-we-research/)).

## Which rice cooker brown rice setting to use

- **Zojirushi NS-ZCC10:** BROWN, plus a separate SEMIBROWN menu with its own water line, per the [NS-ZCC manual](https://store.zojirushi.com/cdn/shop/files/ns-zcc_en.pdf). The manual says the Pre-Washed option can't be selected with Brown or Semibrown.
- **Zojirushi NL-BAC05:** BROWN, meant for short- and medium-grain brown rice, 0.5 to 2 cups, per the [NL-BAC manual](https://store.zojirushi.com/cdn/shop/files/nl-bac_en.pdf).
- **Cuckoo CR-0655F:** Mixed/Brown, which the [CR-0655F manual](https://cuckooamerica.com/cdn/shop/files/CR-0655F_User_Manual.pdf) says to use "when you cook long rice or brown rice," plus a GABA menu "for germinated brown rice." Both take 2 to 4 cups.
- **Hamilton Beach 37548:** WHOLE GRAIN, which the [37548 use and care guide](https://useandcares.hamiltonbeach.com/files/840264200.pdf) says to "use to cook brown rice or other grains."
- **KitchenAid KGC3155BM:** separate brown presets for short- and long-grain rice, per [Reviewed](https://www.reviewed.com/cooking/best-right-now/the-best-rice-cookers).
- **Zojirushi NHS-06:** a single switch and no menu, per the [NHS manual](https://store.zojirushi.com/cdn/shop/files/nhs.pdf), which gives its own brown rice water chart.

## Brown rice water ratio in a rice cooker

1. **Cooker has a BROWN line:** fill to it, using the included cup. Zojirushi's NS-ZCC cup is about 180 ml; Hamilton Beach's 37548 cup is 6 oz (177 ml).
2. **No BROWN line:** Zojirushi's brown rice recipe page says 1.5 cups of water per cup of rice by the same cup (2 cups rice to 3 cups water), slightly more for softer rice.
3. **Basic Zojirushi NHS:** its manual's chart (liters, not a BROWN line) gives 0.2 L of water per cup of brown rice on the NHS-06 (0.4 L for 2 cups), and says to "adjust water quantity to your taste."

The NHS-10 chart runs 0.2 to 0.7 L for 1 to 3 cups and the NHS-18 0.6 to 1.4 L for 2 to 6. The NS-ZCC manual says brown water "can be adjusted according to your preference."

### Where the sources disagree on brown rice water

[America's Test Kitchen](http://www.americastestkitchen.com/equipment-reviews/2328-rice-cookers) cooked medium-grain brown rice in every model it tested and found that at the brands' own ratios "the resulting rice was often too wet." Its testers preferred the amount of water the cooker calls for with long-grain white rice. In the Zojirushi NL-BAC05 specifically, ATK says it had the best brown rice results when it reduced the water the directions called for.

Recipe blogs don't settle it. Fifteen uses equal parts water for long-grain rice soaked at least 30 minutes and at least twice as much unsoaked, with a 160 ml cup. [Hungry Huy](https://www.hungryhuy.com/brown-rice-in-a-rice-cooker/) uses 10 fl oz of water per standard US dry cup of brown rice and says it doesn't use the scoop that came with the cooker. Those ratios can't be compared to a Zojirushi or Hamilton Beach line because the cups differ; see [rice cooker cup size](/guides/rice-cooker-cup-size/). For white, jasmine and basmati, see the [rice cooker water ratio guide](/guides/rice-cooker-water-ratio/).

Zojirushi's FAQ explains the spread: micom cookers are programmed to soak the rice before heating and steam it after, and "longer soaking time is needed for brown rice." A one-switch cooker like the NHS skips that, which is why its manual says about 40 minutes. The NS-ZCC manual lists regular white rice at 48 to 55 minutes. Cuckoo's CR-0655F time table doesn't say which row plain brown rice on Mixed/Brown follows: it lists "Mixed Rice" at 55 to 68 minutes and "Brown GABA" at 83 to 91.

Times are estimates. Zojirushi's NS-ZCC figures assume 120 V, a 68 °F room and 64.4 °F water. [Consumer Reports](https://www.yahoo.com/news/best-rice-cookers-consumer-reports-135809338.html) (2022) timed brown at about an hour in the Instant Zest and "a whopping 90 minutes" in a Zojirushi NP-GBC05, and about 55 minutes in an Aroma ARC-954SBD. [Reviewed](https://www.reviewed.com/cooking/best-right-now/the-best-rice-cookers) says the Toshiba TRCS02's brown rice feature takes about 1 hour 20 minutes.

## How to cook brown rice in a rice cooker, step by step

1. **Measure** the rice with the cup that came with the cooker and level it (Zojirushi recipe page).
2. **Rinse** or not. Zojirushi's recipe page rinses once; the NHS manual says rinse for dry, fluffy rice and skip it for stickier rice.
3. **Fill** water to the BROWN line in the inner pan (NS-ZCC manual; Zojirushi recipe page).
4. **Wipe** the outside of the pan dry before setting it in the cooker (Zojirushi recipe page).
5. **Choose** BROWN, Mixed/Brown or WHOLE GRAIN and press start. On the NHS, leave the steaming tray out (NHS manual).
6. **Rest** a conventional cooker's rice before opening: 15 minutes per Zojirushi's recipe page, about 10 per the NHS manual. Zojirushi says micom and induction models can be opened and fluffed at the end signal.

## No brown setting? Use these limits

Zojirushi's brown rice recipe page gives a method for cookers without a BROWN menu: keep the rice to "2/3 of the rice cooker's capacity to prevent from overflowing," use 1.5 times as much water, soak 30 minutes, then press start and rest 15 minutes after.

Brown capacity is lower than white even on a cooker built for it. The NHS manual caps brown at 1 to 2 cups in the 3-cup NHS-06 and warns that "adding too much rice and/or water may cause the rice to overflow."

[The Woks of Life](https://thewoksoflife.com/wprm_print/how-to-cook-brown-rice) offers a looser version: fill water slightly above the cup line, soak at least 20 minutes (ideally 90), and leave the lid shut an extra 10 minutes after it's done. That's one blog's method, not a manufacturer instruction.

## Using the delay timer and soaking with brown rice

The NS-ZCC manual's shortest Brown timer setting is 1 hour 51 minutes and the longest is 13 hours. It says it's "preferable to not set the timer for more than 13 hours" so the rice doesn't degenerate, and to take care in warm rooms.

The NL-BAC manual allows 1 hour 45 minutes to 13 hours on BROWN and says to stay under 13 hours, especially in summer, so the soaking rice doesn't spoil. It rules out the timer entirely for germinated brown rice.

## GABA, germinated and semi-brown rice settings

GABA (germinated) brown rice is rice soaked warm until it starts to sprout. Zojirushi's FAQ says its GABA brown setting soaks at 104 °F for 2 hours.

Cuckoo's CR-0655F manual works in two steps. Pre-germination happens in a separate container: "soak brown rice for 16 hours in water," never longer, and cut the time in hot weather because odor may develop. The GABA menu then adds its own germination time of 0, 2, 4 or 6 hours before cooking. Set it to 0 to cook already-germinated rice; the delay timer only works at 0.

The manual says to skip the pre-soak if you prefer a smaller germ. Cuckoo also makes health claims for GABA rice that we could not verify.

The NL-BAC manual cooks germinated brown rice on WHITE or SUSHI, with no timer and no soak over 30 minutes. Semi-brown 30% rice goes on BROWN with water slightly below the BROWN line. The NS-ZCC has a dedicated SEMIBROWN menu and line, listed at 55 to 57 minutes in the 5.5-cup model.

## Keeping brown rice warm: what the manuals say

Zojirushi's NHS manual (the NHS-06 has no keep warm; the NHS-10 and NHS-18 do) is blunt: "Do not use the keep warm function as brown rice may become dry or spoil in few hours." The NS-ZCC manual says "Do not keep food except white rice warm," and not to keep rice warm more than 12 hours.

Zojirushi's own NL-BAC manual disagrees: it allows Regular Keep Warm on BROWN for up to 12 hours, but not Extended Keep Warm. Cuckoo's CR-0655F manual says the warming status of mixed/brown rice "is worse than plain rice. Do not keep it warm if at all possible."

ATK also held brown rice on warm for 12 hours in its top three cookers, so follow your own cooker's manual. Engadget saw some overdone spots on the bottom of brown rice from a Hamilton Beach cooker (a different model from the 37548), especially after time on warm.

[FoodSafety.gov](https://www.foodsafety.gov/keep-food-safe/4-steps-to-food-safety) says to keep food at 140 °F or above after cooking and to refrigerate perishable food within 2 hours, and its [minimum temperatures chart](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures) lists leftovers at 165 °F. The [FDA's Bad Bug Book](https://www.fda.gov/media/83271/download) says the vomiting type of Bacillus cereus illness most often involves rice and other starchy foods. More in [how long rice can stay in a rice cooker](/guides/how-long-can-rice-stay-in-rice-cooker/).

## If brown rice comes out wet, hard or scorched

- **Wet:** ATK found brand ratios for medium-grain brown rice often too wet; try less water, down to the long-grain white amount.
- **Inconsistent:** Consumer Reports' tester saw "a lot more variation in taste, texture, and cooking time with brown rice" than with white.
- **Underdone:** Engadget wanted a slightly longer brown cook on the Cuckoo CR-0655F, and CR found some Aroma ARC-954SBD batches slightly undercooked.
