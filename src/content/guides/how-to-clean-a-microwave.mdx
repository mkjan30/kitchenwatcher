To clean a microwave, unplug it, then steam a bowl of water (lemon optional) at full power for 3 to 5 minutes to soften dried-on food. Wipe the cavity with a damp cloth and mild detergent, including the ceiling. Wash the turntable and roller ring in warm soapy water. Keep sprays, abrasives, bleach and ammonia off the door and controls. That routine comes from Breville's and IKEA's manuals, GE and LG support, and the FDA.

## How to clean a microwave inside, step by step

1. **Unplug it.** Breville's BMO650 instruction book (linked in our sources) says to remove the plug before cleaning, IKEA's GATEBO manual says to disconnect the power, and [GE](https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=17377) says to unplug to reduce the risk of shock.
2. **Steam off dried-on food.** GE says to heat one cup of water at full power for three minutes, then let it stand five more minutes before cleaning. [LG](https://www.lg.com/us/support/help-library/lg-microwave-how-to-cleaning-your-microwave-CT40000141-1366666908230) says to boil a cup of water for 2 to 3 minutes for stubborn stains.
3. **Wipe the cavity.** Breville says to wipe splatters with a damp cloth and use mild detergent if the cavity is very dirty. The [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/radiation-emitting-products/resources-you-radiation-emitting-products/microwave-ovens) says water and mild detergent are enough and a special microwave cleaner is not necessary. It also says to avoid scouring pads and steel wool. GE allows a plastic pad or scrubber on baked-on food.
4. **Do the ceiling too.** IKEA's manual says to keep the waveguide cover clean, because food residue on it can cause arcing or fires, and not to remove it. If you already see sparks, read [why a microwave sparks](/guides/why-is-my-microwave-sparking/).
5. **Dry it, and keep the control panel dry.** Breville says not to let the control panel get wet and to leave the door open while cleaning it, to prevent the oven from accidentally turning on.

The sections below cover where the makers' instructions differ.

## How to clean a microwave with lemon: Breville vs IKEA

Only two of the manuals we read give a lemon method, and they disagree on the amount and the time. The table adds GE, LG and the FDA, so every maker-sourced cleaning instruction we found sits side by side.

| Source (manual or support page) | Steam mix | Power and time | Standing time | Turntable care | Cleaners to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Breville BMO650 book | 1 cup water plus juice of one lemon, deep bowl (manufacturer instruction) | 100% for 5 minutes (manufacturer instruction) | Not published | Let cool to room temperature; warm soapy water, rinse, dry thoroughly | Sprays and harsh cleaners on the door, abrasive cleaners, corrosive chemicals |
| IKEA GATEBO manual | Half a lemon in 300 ml water (manufacturer instruction) | 100% for 3 minutes (manufacturer instruction) | Not published | Accessories in soapy water | Harsh abrasives, metal scrapers on door glass, metal scouring pads |
| GE cleaning page | 1 cup water (manufacturer instruction) | Full power for 3 minutes (manufacturer instruction) | 5 minutes (manufacturer instruction) | Not published | Oven cleaner, abrasives, ammonia, bleach |
| LG support page | 1 cup water for stubborn soil; add lemon juice or vinegar for odors, no amount given (manufacturer instruction) | Boil 2 to 3 minutes for stubborn soil; no time given for the odor mix (manufacturer instruction) | Not published | Mild sudsy water or dishwasher, reinstall dry | Benzene, thinners |
| FDA microwave page | Not published | Not published | Not published | Not published | Scouring pads, steel wool, abrasives |

Breville's version is for odors: a cup of water and the juice of one lemon at 100% for 5 minutes, then wipe and dry with a soft cloth. IKEA's is for stubborn dirt: half a lemon in 300 ml of water at 100% for 3 minutes, then wipe with a soft dry cloth. Follow your own manual where it has a method.

Popular guides add a rest that neither manual mentions. [Bob Vila](https://bobvila.com/articles/how-to-clean-a-microwave) uses the juice of two lemons in 2 cups of water for 2 to 3 minutes, then about 10 minutes with the door closed. [Homes & Gardens](https://homesandgardens.com/kitchens/how-to-clean-a-microwave), quoting a New York cleaning firm, runs 1 cup of water with lemon for 5 minutes, then 15 minutes standing. [Food Network](https://www.foodnetwork.com/how-to/packages/food-network-essentials/how-to-clean-a-microwave) steams for 3 to 4 minutes, then keeps the door closed about 5 minutes more. GE's 5-minute stand is the only maker-sourced rest we found.

## How to clean a microwave with vinegar: thin maker support

Of the maker sources we read, only LG's support page mentions vinegar. It says boiling a cup of water with some lemon juice or vinegar removes odors, and gives no amounts. Breville's, IKEA's and Toshiba's manuals and GE's cleaning page don't mention it.

The ratios you'll see elsewhere come from cleaning sites, and they disagree. Bob Vila mixes half a cup of water with half a cup of white vinegar for 2 to 3 minutes. Homes & Gardens, quoting a cleaning-company president, uses 1 cup of water to half a cup of vinegar for 5 minutes. Food Network warns that heated vinegar gives off a strong smell. If you use vinegar, treat these as those sites' recipes, not manufacturer advice.

## Baking soda, dish soap and the cleaners GE rules out

GE's cleaning page says mild dishwashing liquid on a damp cloth is the default, baking soda and water may be used on painted or stainless interiors (no ratio given), and microwave-interior cleaners may be used. Its [stainless interior page](https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=17378) adds a sudsy cloth for grease and a soap-filled plastic pad for heavy soil.

What not to use, per the makers:

- Commercial oven cleaner on any part (GE).
- Abrasive cleaners, scouring pads or steel wool (GE, FDA, Breville; IKEA warns metal scouring pads can shed pieces onto electrical parts).
- Corrosive chemicals on the door and sealing surfaces, which Breville says will damage the oven and may result in radiation leaks.
- Cleaners with ammonia or bleach, which GE says can make the plastic brittle.
- Benzene or thinners (LG).

This is where Homes & Gardens and GE part ways: Homes & Gardens says Windex and bleach can be used if rinsed off, while GE says no ammonia or bleach on any part.

## How to clean the microwave turntable and roller ring

Breville's book says to remove the glass turntable, wash it in warm soapy water, then rinse and dry it thoroughly. Wipe the cavity floor and wash the roller in mild soapy water. Breville says cleaning both regularly helps the turntable rotate properly without excess noise, and the roller must go back in its proper position.

Whether the tray can go in the dishwasher depends on the model. LG says its turntable and support are dishwasher safe and should be reinstalled only when dry. Breville's book describes hand washing only, so check your manual before using the dishwasher.

Let the glass tray cool first: Breville's book says to let the turntable cool to room temperature before washing it or putting it in cold water.

## How to clean the door seals, door glass and control panel

Breville, IKEA and Toshiba all single out the surfaces where the door meets the oven. Toshiba's [EM131A5C-BS booklet](https://images.homedepot-static.com/catalog/pdfImages/fb/fb6df202-147c-4227-8107-017db04f8ed2.pdf) and Breville say not to let soil or cleaner residue build up on the sealing surfaces, and Toshiba says to clean them only with mild, nonabrasive soap on a sponge or soft cloth.

IKEA's manual warns that harsh abrasives or sharp metal scrapers can scratch the door glass, which may make it shatter. That conflicts with Bob Vila's advice to use a melamine sponge on the inside of the door glass. Breville says sprays and harsh cleaners may stain, streak or dull the door surface. GE says to wipe the controls with a soft damp cloth, dry them immediately, and never spray solution directly onto them.

Breville says not to operate the oven if the door or door seals are damaged and to call its support line. The FDA says not to use an oven whose door is bent, warped or otherwise damaged or doesn't close firmly, and to contact the manufacturer if the hinges, latches or seals are damaged. The [radiation safety guide](/guides/is-microwave-radiation-safe/) covers why.

## How to clean the outside, vents and over-the-range filters

Breville says to wipe the outside with a damp cloth and not let water seep into the ventilation openings. LG says to use mild detergent on the exterior and a neutral glass cleaner on the front glass, sprayed onto a non-abrasive cloth rather than onto the oven.

Over-the-range models have filters. [Whirlpool's help page](https://producthelp.whirlpool.com/Cooking/Microwaves/Over-the-Range_Microwave/Cleaning_and_Filters/Exterior/Cleaning_the_Filters_(Grease_and_Charcoal)_-_Over-the-Range_Microwave) says to clean the grease filter monthly with mild soap and water. Most can go in the dishwasher, but check the owner's manual. The charcoal filter can't be cleaned and should be replaced about every 6 months.

## How often should you clean a microwave?

The makers don't agree, and the range runs from after each use to weekly. Breville says to wipe the cavity after each use, because food residue, grease or fat left inside can make it overheat, smoke or catch fire. If that happens, see [what to do in a microwave fire](/guides/microwave-fire-what-to-do/).

GE says to clean soil as soon as it appears. LG recommends weekly cleaning. Breville adds that not keeping the oven clean could damage the surface, shorten its life and possibly create a hazard.

## Is steam cleaning a microwave safe?

Mostly, if you watch for superheated water. The FDA warns that water heated past boiling may not look like it is boiling, and that it can erupt when disturbed. It says this happens when water is heated by itself in a clean cup, as in the plain-water steam methods above. Breville's superheated-liquids warning says not to use straight-sided containers with narrow necks and to let the container stand in the oven for a short time before removing it.

The FDA says some ovens should not be run empty, and Toshiba lists running empty as a cause of arcing. So don't switch the oven on empty to dry it after cleaning. Breville and IKEA say to dry the cavity with a soft cloth. More on this is in [what not to put in a microwave](/guides/what-not-to-put-in-a-microwave/).
