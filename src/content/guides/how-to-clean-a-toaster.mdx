How to clean a toaster, per the GE, Breville, KitchenAid and Dualit instructions: unplug it and let it cool, slide out and empty the crumb tray (KitchenAid says after every use), loosen stuck crumbs with a soft dry brush, and wipe the outside with a damp cloth. Never immerse it, put it in a dishwasher, or push metal into the slots.

One point the manuals don't agree on: GE's manual says never to turn the toaster upside down and shake it, while Breville's booklets and Cuisinart's CPT-180 manual say to do exactly that. Follow your own model's manual. Everything below comes from manufacturer manuals and support pages, linked where cited.

## How to clean a toaster in 5 steps

1. **Unplug it and let it cool completely.** GE, Breville, KitchenAid and Dualit all say this. KitchenAid adds that the crumb tray is hot right after a toasting cycle.
2. **Slide out the crumb tray and empty it over a bin.** KitchenAid says to grasp the center of the tray and shake the crumbs into a waste container. GE's 4-slice model has a tray on each side.
3. **Loosen stuck crumbs with a soft dry brush.** Dualit recommends a soft brush so crumbs fall into the tray, and says not to use any chemicals or liquids inside the slots. KitchenAid rules out liquid cleaning products on its toasters.
4. **Wipe the outside with a damp soft cloth, then dry it.** GE says a damp cloth; Breville says a slightly dampened soft cloth, then polish dry; KitchenAid says a soft, damp cotton cloth and no paper towels.
5. **Put the tray back until it locks.** KitchenAid says press it in until it clicks; Breville says slide it in until it locks. GE, Breville and KitchenAid all say never to run the toaster without the tray in place.

Sources: [GE manual 49-9000001](https://pdf.lowes.com/productdocuments/e17a288a-af8b-4410-ade3-d6dfb6cb7764/65455388.pdf), [Breville BTA720/BTA730 booklet](https://assets.breville.com/Instruction-Booklets/ANZ/BTA730_IB_A13_WEB.pdf) (Australia/New Zealand edition), [KitchenAid toaster cleaning](https://producthelp.kitchenaid.com/Countertop_Appliances/Other_Countertop_Appliances/Toasters/2_and_4_Slice_Toaster/Cleaning_and_Maintenance/Cleaning_-_Toaster), and Dualit's "Classic Toaster - Be Aware" help page.

## Toaster crumb tray: how often to empty it and how to wash it

KitchenAid recommends emptying the toaster crumb tray after each use, and says the tray on its 2-slice, 4-slice and [Pro Line toasters](https://producthelp.kitchenaid.com/Countertop_Appliances/Other_Countertop_Appliances/Toasters/Pro_Line_Toaster/Cleaning_and_Maintenance/How_to_Clean_the_Pro_Line_Toaster) is hand-wash only. That's the only interval in any manual or support page we read. The manuals from Breville, Cuisinart and Hamilton Beach say "regularly" or give no schedule, so we won't invent a number of days.

The reason they all stress it is fire. Breville's booklet warns that failure to clean the crumb tray regularly may result in a fire hazard and damage the toaster. Dualit says emptying the tray is vital to avoid a risk of fire. A [Hamilton Beach manual](https://useandcares.hamiltonbeach.com/files/840265801.pdf) for model 22614-SAU, a 220–240 V model not sold in the US, says regular cleaning will also reduce the risk of fire hazard.

Only one manual for the six models on our [best toasters](/best/toasters/) page answers the dishwasher question. Black+Decker's [manual for the T2569C and T2569BC](https://web.archive.org/web/20250421051740/https://s7cdn.spectrumbrands.com/~/media/SmallAppliancesUS/Black%20and%20Decker/Files/T2569%20Use%20and%20Care%20Manual.pdf) (the Canadian versions of the T2569; we couldn't find the US T2569B manual) says to wash the tray in warm, sudsy water and that it is not dishwasher safe. The Breville, Cuisinart and GE manuals we read don't mention the tray and the dishwasher at all, so hand-wash it unless your own manual says otherwise. If your toaster smokes, GE's troubleshooting chart says to press Cancel and empty the tray if it's full; the causes are covered in [why is my toaster smoking](/guides/why-is-my-toaster-smoking/).

## How to clean inside a toaster without damaging the elements

Inside the slots, use dry methods only. Dualit's advice is a soft brush to loosen stuck crumbs so they drop into the tray.

Metal is out. Breville says not to clean with metal scouring pads, because pieces can break off and touch electrical parts, creating a shock hazard; Hamilton Beach says the same. GE says never to use scouring pads, abrasive cleaners, or aggressive liquids such as petrol or acetone, and not to insert foreign objects into the slots.

Cuisinart's CPT-180 manual adds: never insert any hard or sharp instruments into the slot. If a slice is stuck, GE's troubleshooting chart says to unplug the toaster, let it cool and never use a metal object; [America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/equipment_reviews/1949-long-slot-toasters) suggests wooden tongs or bamboo chopsticks. The full method is in [how to get toast out of a toaster](/guides/how-to-get-toast-out-of-toaster/).

## Should you turn a toaster upside down to shake out crumbs? The manuals disagree

GE's manual for the G9TMA2SSPSS and G9TMA4SSPSS (49-9000001) carries a caution: "Do not hold the appliance upside down and do not shake it" to remove crumbs, bread or other items. Breville's BTA720/BTA730 booklet says the opposite: to remove any additional crumbs, turn the toaster upside down over a trash bin and shake crumbs free. Breville's [US booklet for the BTA820XL](https://assets.ajmadison.com/ajmadison/itemdocs/BTA820-840XL-Instructions.pdf) has the same step. Cuisinart's US booklets for the [CPT-180](https://images.homedepot-static.com/catalog/pdfImages/65/6531aaae-4ca9-4ef2-8b24-b362a7e0ffe1.pdf) and the [CPT-122](https://images.homedepot-static.com/catalog/pdfImages/6e/6e5ae4b6-2eaf-4d50-82b7-d521f5345e66.pdf) both say to turn the toaster upside down and gently shake to remove pieces of bread, and the Hamilton Beach 22614-SAU manual says to turn it upside down and shake to remove food lodged in the slots.

KitchenAid sits in between: it shakes only the removed tray. Bob Vila and Sage (Breville's UK brand) both say to shake the toaster, and neither mentions GE's caution.

Our advice: follow your own model's manual, and if it says not to, don't. On the Breville side, the [US instruction book for the BTA720 and BTA730](https://assets.breville.com/BTA720/BTA720_BTA730_USCM_IB_F26_LR.pdf) (Issue F26) gives the same upside-down step as the Australia/New Zealand booklet, and so does the US BTA820XL booklet.

## Can you clean a toaster with vinegar?

Not inside. None of the manuals or support pages read (GE, Breville, Cuisinart, KitchenAid, Dualit, Hamilton Beach) mentions vinegar at all. Dualit says not to use any chemicals or liquids inside the toaster slots, and KitchenAid says no liquid cleaning products.

The advice to clean a toaster with vinegar comes from general cleaning articles, and only for the outside. [Bob Vila](https://www.bobvila.com/articles/how-to-clean-a-toaster/) suggests equal parts water and vinegar with a drop of dish soap, sprayed onto a microfiber cloth for stainless exteriors; a blog post from Sage, Breville's UK brand, mentions diluted vinegar on stainless models without a ratio, and says to keep water and cleaning products out of the inside. Neither ties the advice to a specific model's manual.

So the evidence for vinegar is thin. On the exterior, a damp cloth is what the manuals say, unless yours allows a cleaner: Dualit permits a stainless steel cleaner on the Classic's central cover, but not on its die-cast end castings, where it warns the chemicals could cause discoloration.

## Cleaning the outside: stainless steel, plastic and cord

- **Body:** GE says a damp cloth, and marks "never immerse the appliance in water or a dishwasher" as a danger.
- **Grease:** KitchenAid says to wipe grease or oil splatters away immediately with a clean, damp cotton cloth, and to avoid paper towels and abrasive cleansers.
- **Cleaners:** Breville says not to use harsh abrasive, caustic or oven cleaners. Cuisinart's CPT-180 manual says no abrasive cleansers, and to apply any cleansing agent to the cloth, not to the toaster.
- **Cord:** GE says a damaged cord must be replaced by a service center. GE and Breville both say never to immerse the cord or plug.

## How often to clean a toaster

Empty the crumb tray after each use, per KitchenAid; the other manufacturers say "regularly." Bob Vila suggests every few weeks (weekly with heavy daily use), and Sage's blog suggests a quick clean once a week with daily use. Sage is Breville's UK brand, but no manual we read gives that schedule, so treat it as a rule of thumb.
