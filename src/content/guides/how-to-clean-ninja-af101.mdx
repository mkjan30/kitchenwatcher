To clean a Ninja AF101, unplug it and let it cool, then put the basket and crisper plate in the dishwasher or soak them in warm soapy water, and wipe the main unit with a damp cloth. ## What SharkNinja says

Per SharkNinja's AF100-series FAQ (the AF101 is an AF100-series model):

- The basket and crisper plate are dishwasher safe.
- If food is stuck on, soak them in warm, soapy water.
- Don't use abrasive cleaners, steel wool or scouring pads on them.
- Wipe the main unit and control panel with a damp cloth. It doesn't go in the dishwasher or in water.
- Unplug before cleaning.

Everything below builds on that. The times and residue numbers are mine.

## Step by step: basket and crisper plate

1. Unplug the AF101 and let the basket cool until you can hold it bare-handed.
2. Pull the crisper plate out of the basket. If it's stuck, let it cool more rather than prying with metal.
3. Pour off loose grease into a container for the trash, not down the sink.
4. Dishwasher: load both parts. Hand wash: warm water, dish soap, soft sponge. For anything stuck, soak for 20 minutes first.
5. For grease that's baked on over several cooks, spread a paste of baking soda and a little water, leave it 10 minutes, then wipe with a soft sponge and rinse.
6. Dry both parts fully before they go back in.

## Step by step: the heating element

Grease on the element is what makes an air fryer smoke and smell. The AF101's element sits in the top of the cooking chamber, so you reach it from below.

1. Unplug and let the unit cool completely. The element should be cold to the touch.
2. Remove the basket. Turn the base on its back or side on a towel so you can see the element.
3. Brush off loose bits with a soft, dry brush.
4. Wipe the element and the surrounding shield with a cloth that's damp, not dripping. Add a drop of dish soap to the cloth for grease.
5. Wipe again with a clean damp cloth, then let it air dry before plugging in.

Don't spray cleaner into the chamber, don't pour water in, and don't use a metal scraper on the element.

## What not to use

- Steel wool, scouring pads or abrasive powders on the basket or plate (SharkNinja says no).
- Bleach, ammonia or oven cleaner. LoveToKnow also warns against bleach, ammonia and disinfectant sprays on Ninja air fryers ([LoveToKnow](https://www.lovetoknow.com/home/cleaning/clean-ninja-air-fryer)).
- Metal utensils to pry the crisper plate loose.
- Water anywhere in the base.

## Safety notes

- Always unplug first and wait for it to cool. TechGearLab measured the AF101's hottest exterior point at 160°F during cooking ([TechGearLab](https://www.techgearlab.com/topics/kitchen/best-air-fryer)), and the basket and element run hotter than the outside.
- Let every part dry fully before the next cook.
- If the element is damaged or the unit smokes after a clean element and basket, stop using it and contact SharkNinja.
