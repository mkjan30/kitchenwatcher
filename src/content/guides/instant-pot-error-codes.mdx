Instant Pot error codes fall into two groups: ones you can fix and hardware faults. Per Instant's Rio Plus Wide, Rio Wide and Duo Evo Plus manuals, C7 or NoPr means too little liquid, the valve on Vent, or a failed heating element. Lid means the lid is wrong for the program, Burn means the pot bottom is too hot, and PrSE means pressure in a non-pressure program. C1, C2 or C6 means a faulty sensor, which is a Consumer Care call.

Everything here comes from those three Instant manuals and Instant's FAQ, plus America's Test Kitchen's test notes and iFixit's repair wiki, credited where used. Nobody at Kitchen Watcher triggered or reproduced any of these codes; [how we research](/how-we-research/) explains the method.

## Instant Pot error code chart by manual

Each cell below is what the named manual says. "Listed" means the code appears in that manual's error-code table.

| C6H, C6L | Faulty sensor | Contact Consumer Care | Listed | Not listed | Listed | Yes |

## Instant Pot C7 error or NoPr: the cooker can't build pressure

C7 and NoPr share one row in all three manuals, so they mean the same thing: the cooker isn't building pressure. The [Rio Plus Wide manual](https://www.qvc.com/footers/cd/pdf/K89432_Manual.pdf) and the [Duo Evo Plus manual](https://www.williams-sonoma.com/netstorage/pdf/4629578-2205325-Instant-Pot-Duo-Evo-Plus-6QT-8QT-Manual.pdf) give three causes, in this order:

1. The heat element has failed. Contact Consumer Care.
2. There isn't enough liquid. Add thin, water-based liquid by pot size: 1 cup for a 3 qt, 1½ cups for a 6 qt, 2 cups for an 8 qt (Rio Plus Wide manual).
3. The quick release is set to Vent. Move the steam release switch to Seal.

The [Rio Wide manual](https://media-www.canadiantire.ca/manual/product/0439809/0439809-1-76932d79-d779-4ab7-9028-c556b46ae86c.pdf) lists only the first two causes and gives one liquid amount, 1½ cups. Since the fix for a failed element is a call and the other two are free, check the liquid and the switch first.

[America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/equipment_reviews/2242-multicookers) got a C7 on an Instant Pot Duo Crisp 6.5-quart with Ultimate Lid while pressure-cooking beef stew. ATK says it found nothing about C7 in that model's manual. After it added more liquid and restarted, ATK reports tender beef and fully cooked vegetables.

For minimum liquid by size, see [how much water goes in a pressure cooker](/guides/how-much-water-in-pressure-cooker/). If the switch is on Seal and steam still escapes, go to [Instant Pot not sealing](/guides/instant-pot-not-sealing/).

## Instant Pot lid error: what Lid on the display means

All three manuals say the same thing: the lid is not in the correct position for the selected program. The fix is to open and close the lid. The manuals add that you shouldn't use a lid at all with Sauté.

A Lid code is about the lid's position for the program, which is a different problem from a lid that won't open after cooking. If the lid won't open, Instant's FAQ says never to force it: release pressure per the recipe and open only once the float valve has dropped. The [float valve guide](/guides/instant-pot-float-valve/) covers a valve that stays up, and [are pressure cookers safe](/guides/are-pressure-cookers-safe/) covers why lids lock.

## Burn, Food Burn or OvHt: the pot bottom is too hot

The manuals define it as high temperature at the bottom of the inner pot. The cooker lowers its own heat to avoid overheating. Starch deposits on the pot bottom may have blocked heat from dissipating, so turn the cooker off, release pressure per the recipe, and inspect the bottom of the pot.

The Duo Evo Plus manual lists OvHt, Burn and Food burn as one code. Instant's FAQ adds three causes: not enough liquid, the steam release not sealed or set to venting, and not deglazing after sautéing. The full fix is on the [Instant Pot burn message](/guides/instant-pot-burn-message/) page.

## PrSE: pressure in a non-pressure program

PrSE means pressure built up while you were running a program that isn't meant to pressurize. All three manuals give the same action, but each words it differently:

- Rio Plus Wide: set the Steam Release switch to Vent.
- Duo Evo Plus: move the quick release switch to the Vent position.
- Rio Wide: set Steam Release to Quick release.

These are the same move with different labels: the Rio Wide manual's own quick release method is to set the steam release switch to Vent.

## C5: too hot, no pot or no water

Of the three manuals, only the Rio Plus Wide's table lists C5. It gives two causes. If the inner pot isn't in the cooker base, press Cancel, wait for the heating element to cool, check the base for foreign objects, reseat the pot and re-enter your settings.

If there's no water in the pot, check the pot bottom for scorching and add thin, water-based liquid by size (1 cup for 3 qt, 1½ cups for 6 qt, 2 cups for 8 qt). The Rio Wide and Duo Evo Plus tables don't list C5 at all.

## C8: wrong inner pot

Only the Duo Evo Plus manual lists C8: the wrong inner pot is in the cooker base. The fix is to insert the correctly sized inner pot with easy-grip handles. We haven't read an Instant Pot Pro manual, so this page doesn't say whether Pro models show C8.

## C1, C2, C6, C6H, C6L: sensor faults

All three manuals call these a faulty sensor and send you to Instant's support team (Consumer Care in the Rio manuals, Customer Care in the Duo Evo Plus manual). The Rio Wide manual lists C1, C2 and C6 only; the Rio Plus Wide and Duo Evo Plus manuals add C6H and C6L.

Instant's FAQ says C6, C6L or C6H can also appear after something spills onto the cooker base. For a water-based spill, let the burner cool completely, wipe the base and element with a dry cloth, turn the Instant Pot upside down on a flat surface and let it air-dry for 72 hours. Then run the [water test](/guides/instant-pot-water-test/). For an oil- or fat-based spill, the FAQ says not to use the appliance and to dispose of it per local regulations.

[iFixit's student-written troubleshooting wiki](https://www.ifixit.com/Wiki/Instant_Pot_IP-DUO80_V2_Troubleshooting) for the Duo 8 qt (IP-DUO80 V2) documents C1 and C2 as temperature-sensor problems and C6 as a pressure-sensor problem. The Rio Wide manual says not to repair, replace or modify components yourself, and that doing so voids the warranty. Treat iFixit as an explanation of the codes, not a repair plan.

## Codes no Instant manual we read confirms

C3, C4, C9, C10, Alt, Pot and the E1 to E19 codes appear in third-party guides; [Tasting Table's list](https://www.tastingtable.com/2157318/what-instant-pot-codes-mean-c-1-2-3-4-5-6-7/), for example, names them without citing a manual. None appears in the three manual tables we read. If you see one, check your model's manual or ask Consumer Care rather than trusting an unsourced list.

Ninja cookers use different messages. ATK reported a "NO PRESSURE" message on a Ninja Foodi XL (OL601) while pressure-cooking beef stew, and got good results after restarting. We couldn't read a Ninja manual, so we don't decode Ninja codes here.

## Messages that aren't errors

- "L0:02" counting up: per Instant's FAQ, an L on some models means Keep Warm, and the time shows how long it has been warming.
- Minor hissing from the steam release vent during cooking: the Rio Plus Wide manual calls this the cooker regulating excess pressure, which is normal.
- Occasional clicking: Instant's FAQ says it's the heating element switching on and off to regulate temperature or pressure.

Venting at the end of a cook is a choice, not a fault; see [natural vs quick release](/guides/natural-release-vs-quick-release/).

## When to stop and contact Instant

Stop and contact Instant Consumer Care for any code the manual sends there: C1, C2, C6, C6H or C6L, a C7 that persists after you've added liquid and set the switch to Seal, and any oil or fat spilled onto the element. The two Rio manuals list contact through Instant's app and its online customer care center. The Rio Wide and Duo Evo Plus manuals give the email support@instantpot.com, and the Duo Evo Plus manual adds the phone line 1-800-828-7280. The Rio Plus Wide manual gives support@instanthome.com instead, so use the address in your own model's manual.

CPSC's recall database shows one Instant Pot pressure cooker recall, [CPSC 15-741](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2015/Instant-Pot-Pressure-Cookers-Recalled-by-Double-Insight). That July 2015 recall covered about 1,000 Bluetooth Instant Pot Smart and Smart-60 cookers (serial numbers 1410 to 1503), because the base's thermal probe could conduct electricity. We found no CPSC recall for the Duo, Rio or Pro lines. Our [pressure cooker recall check](/guides/pressure-cooker-recall-check/) covers the other pressure cooker recalls.
