The Instant Pot water test is a 5-minute pressure cycle with plain water. Per Instant's [Duo Plus getting-started guide](https://www.qvc.com/footers/cd/pdf/K49640_quick_start_guide.pdf) and the [Rio Plus Wide manual](https://www.qvc.com/footers/cd/pdf/K89432_Manual.pdf), add 3 cups of water, lock the lid, select Pressure Cook for 5 minutes, then move the steam release to Vent and open once the float valve drops. The Rio Plus manual also says to turn Keep Warm off. Instant's [Initial Test Run sheet](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/3105/6660/files/Initial_Test_Run.pdf) says it takes about 20 minutes.

## How to do the Instant Pot water test, step by step

If it's your first time using an Instant Pot, this is the test run as the Rio Plus Wide manual and the Duo Plus getting-started guide lay it out. Button names differ by model (see the table below).

1. **Check the lid.** The sealing ring should be seated and the anti-block shield installed. The Rio Plus manual says the anti-block shield must be installed before use.
2. **Add 3 cups of water** (24 oz / 750 mL, per the Duo Plus guide) to the inner pot.
3. **Insert the pot, then close and lock the lid.** On the Duo Plus, the guide says the steam release resets to Seal when the lid closes.
4. **Select Pressure Cook and set 5 minutes.** On the Pro, the manual adds Custom, then High pressure.
5. **Turn Keep Warm off**, per the Rio Plus manual.
6. **Press Start.** The Rio Plus display shows Preheating; the Duo Plus guide says the display shows On after 10 seconds.
7. **Wait for the float valve to rise.** Once it does, the countdown begins (Duo Plus guide).
8. **Wait for the end of the program.** The Rio Plus display shows DO NOT OPEN LID; the Duo Plus shows End or Keep Warm.
9. **Move the steam release to Vent.** The Rio Plus manual says not to put your face, hands or exposed skin over the steam release valve or float valve; the Pro manual warns that pressurized steam releases through the top of the valve and can cause burns.
10. **Open the lid once the float valve drops.** The Rio Plus display alternates OK to Open Lid and End.
11. **Discard the water, then wash and dry the pot**, per the Rio Plus manual.

If you haven't washed the new parts yet, see [how to clean an Instant Pot](/guides/how-to-clean-an-instant-pot/).

## 3 cups or 3 quarts? Instant's manuals disagree

Instant's documents disagree. Three say 3 cups: the Duo Plus getting-started guide, the Rio Plus Wide manual and the Initial Test Run sheet. Two say 3 quarts, four times as much: the 2022 [Duo Plus V4 6-quart US manual](https://www.qvc.com/footers/cd/pdf/K74342_OwnersManual.pdf) ("Add 3 quarts water") and the [Rio Wide 7.5-quart manual](https://media-www.canadiantire.ca/manual/product/0439809/0439809-1-76932d79-d779-4ab7-9028-c556b46ae86c.pdf) ("Use 3 quarts water, but no food"). The [Instant Pot Pro manual](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/3105/6660/files/Instant_Pot_Pro_User_Manual_-_FINAL_f9906a33-b6be-4749-85f2-64abd690e956.pdf) gives no volume at all, only "fill it to the first line (1)".

We can't tell you which is the intended amount. Instant hasn't said, and we haven't asked it yet. {/* VERIFY: ask Instant Customer Care whether the Duo Plus V4 and Rio Wide "3 quarts" is intended or a typo for 3 cups. */}

What we can show is arithmetic of our own, not Instant's. Three quarts fills a 6-quart pot halfway and a 7.5-quart pot to 40%. The Rio Plus manual's fill limit is the "PC MAX — 2/3" line, so either amount stays under it. {/* VERIFY: the Duo Plus V4 manual's own max-fill wording; our text extraction didn't surface it. */}

More water mainly means a longer wait. Instant's sheet says the time to pressurize depends on liquid volume. Follow the manual printed for your model. If you have no manual, 3 cups is what most of Instant's documents say.

## What to expect during an Instant Pot initial test

Instant's Initial Test Run sheet splits the run into three stages. The copy we found is a 220-240V edition.

- **Heating and pressurizing.** The sheet says this "may take 5 – 15 minutes" during the water test. Steam from the float valve hole before the valve pops up is normal. The lid stays removable until the cooker pressurizes, then it locks. The sheet says not to press down on the lid.
- **Cook cycle.** The countdown doesn't start at once: "within 5 minutes of pressurization" the cooker beeps once and the countdown appears. At 00:00 it beeps 10 times.
- **Release.** The sheet says to make sure the float valve has dropped completely before opening, and never to force the lid. With Keep Warm off, the cooker cools faster.

Bloggers' timings are their own, not Instant's. [Amy + Jacky at Pressure Cook Recipes](https://pressurecookrecipes.com/instant-pot-water-test/) say 2 to 3 cups of water and that it will take roughly 13 minutes to reach High Pressure. [Corrie Cooks](https://www.corriecooks.com/instant-pot-water-test/) says to expect 10 to 20 minutes to come to pressure and about 20 minutes in all.

## Do you have to do the Instant Pot water test?

No, says the Pro manual, which asks the question itself. It adds that getting to know the Pro "prepares you for success". Instant's FAQ gives the test a purpose: for food that keeps coming out undercooked or overcooked, it says to "run the intial [sic] test to confirm your unit is operating as intended". If the test fails, the FAQ says to contact Instant's customer care team.

Instant's FAQ also tells owners to run the water test again after water-based liquid spills onto the heating element. First let it cool completely, wipe the inside of the base and the element with a dry cloth, and air-dry the cooker upside down for 72 hours. If the spill was oil or fat, the FAQ says not to use the appliance at all.

## If the Instant Pot test run fails

- **Steam from the sides of the lid:** stop and check the sealing ring once it has cooled, per the Initial Test Run sheet and the Rio Plus manual.
- **Steady steam from the valve:** the Rio Plus manual says to turn the cooker off if steam streams from the steam release valve or float valve for longer than 3 minutes.
- **Float valve never rises:** the test-run steps need the lid locked and the steam release on Seal. Beyond that, the Pro manual's troubleshooting table lists food debris on the float valve or its silicone cap, too little liquid, and a damaged or missing silicone cap. See [Instant Pot not sealing](/guides/instant-pot-not-sealing/) and [Instant Pot float valve](/guides/instant-pot-float-valve/).
- **A code on the display:** see [Instant Pot error codes](/guides/instant-pot-error-codes/).
- **Still failing:** for a float valve that won't rise or no heat in the pot, the Pro manual's troubleshooting table says to perform the initial test run, record your findings and contact Customer Care.

Never force the lid. The Pro manual says the float valve must be down before you remove it, because the contents are under extreme pressure.
