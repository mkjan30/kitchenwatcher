Our search of the CPSC recall database on November 27, 2026 found no recall covering a current countertop microwave from a major brand. The only recall it returned for an oven the CPSC record calls countertop is Kenmore Elite 20-020 (2019, about 700 units). Most microwave recalls cover over-the-range or built-in units: Whirlpool 02-018 and 16-253, GE 08-110 and Samsung 10-031. Find your model and serial, then match them below.

## Is my microwave recalled? How to check in 4 steps

1. **Find the label.** Where the CPSC records say it is:
   - Whirlpool over-the-range (16-253): inside the unit, above the oven cavity on the left.
   - Whirlpool, KitchenAid and Kenmore over-the-range (02-018): open the door.
   - GE, GE Profile and Kenmore combination wall ovens (08-110): the left interior wall of the microwave.
   - Kenmore Elite countertop (20-020): the data plate on the back.
2. **Write down the brand, model and full serial number.** The serial matters most. GE's recall FAQ says not every unit with a recalled model number is included.
3. **Match both against the table below,** then search the [CPSC Recall API](https://www.saferproducts.gov/RestWebServices/Recall?format=json&ProductName=microwave) or SaferProducts.gov by brand.
4. **Confirm with the maker's recall line,** listed in each section below, with the model and serial in front of you.

## Whirlpool microwave recall: 16-253 and 02-018

### 16-253 (August 25, 2016)

The CPSC record covers models WMH53520CS, WMH53520CW, WMH53520CB, WMH53520CE, WMH53520CH, WMH73521CS, WMH73521CW, WMH73521CB, WMH73521CE and WMH73521CH, all with serials TR33500000 to TR34899999. About 15,200 were sold from January 2014 to April 2016 for $370 to $470.

Internal arcing can ignite a plastic part; Whirlpool had five reports, including one home fire and two fires involving cabinets. Stop using it, unplug it and call Whirlpool at 800-990-6254 (8 a.m. to 8 p.m. ET, weekdays) for a free replacement.

### 02-018 (October 16, 2001)

About 1.8 million Whirlpool, KitchenAid and Kenmore microwave-hood units with serials beginning XC, sold from January 1998 to September 2001. They can overheat and catch fire, with seven fires and no injuries reported. The remedy is a free repair; the 2001 record lists Whirlpool at (800) 785-8897, a number we have not confirmed still works. The record says plainly: "Countertop microwaves are not included in this recall."

Two more Whirlpool records touch microwaves: 04-577 (built-in GSC and KitchenAid KEHC/KEMC combos, wiring) and 15-200 (Jenn-Air combos, where the hazard is the roller rack, not the microwave).

### The "21-172" Whirlpool recall that doesn't exist

At least one appliance-repair blog cites a 2021 recall, "CPSC #21-172", of about 1.8 million Whirlpool, Maytag, KitchenAid and Amana over-the-range microwaves. [CPSC 21-172](https://www.saferproducts.gov/RestWebServices/Recall?format=json&RecallNumber=21172) is a July 2021 recall of Birkenstock kids' sandals. A search by Whirlpool in the recall title returns only 16-253, 15-200, 04-577 and 02-018 for microwaves. The 1.8 million figure matches 02-018, from 2001.

## Kenmore microwave recall: 20-020, plus two shared recalls

Kenmore is a Sears store brand, and other companies built the ovens. A Kenmore owner has to check three recalls.

- **20-020, Kenmore Elite countertop (made by Galanz).** 1,000-W convection model 204.77603610, made April 27, 2017, serials 17042700001 to 17042700684 (they begin 170427). Incorrect wiring lets the exterior exceed 183 °F; no injuries reported. Sold only at Sears and Sears Hometown and Outlet stores, May 2017 to July 2018, for about $350. Free in-home repair from Sears at 800-659-7026 (7 a.m. to 7 p.m. CT, weekdays).
- **02-018 (Whirlpool-built).** Kenmore over-the-range units with serials beginning XC.
- **08-110 (GE-built).** Kenmore combination wall ovens with model numbers starting 911 (911.41485991 to 94, 911.41489991 to 94, 911.47692100 and others) and serials beginning 0 to 3, per [GE's recall FAQ](https://www.geappliances.com/ge/recall/micro_oven_combo/faq.htm).

The Sears contact details date from 2019 and earlier. The 08-110 record now lists "Transform for Kenmore branded products" at the same 800-659-7026 number; we have not called it to confirm it still answers.

## GE, Samsung and other brand microwave recalls

**GE 08-110.** About 92,000 GE, GE Profile and Kenmore built-in combination wall ovens, sold from 2000 to 2003. The door switch can overheat and ignite plastic parts; the CPSC lists 35 minor property-damage incidents and one cabinet fire, while GE's FAQ counts 36 incidents (22 complaints and 14 property-damage claims). GE's FAQ also says the recall does not affect its countertop or over-the-range microwaves.

GE's FAQ offers a free repair, or instead a $300 rebate toward a new GE unit or $600 toward a GE Profile unit; for Kenmore, the FAQ names Sears and a $300 rebate, and the CPSC record now names Transform. The lower oven can still be used. GE's sources disagree on the phone number and hours, so try both if one fails:

- **GE FAQ answers:** 888-240-2745, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET weekdays.
- **Block at the bottom of the same GE page:** 1-888-569-1588, same hours.
- **CPSC record:** 888-240-2745, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. ET weekdays and 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. Saturday.

**Samsung 10-031.** About 43,000 SMH9151 over-the-range units made January to May 2009, plus some June units. If an installation bolt touches an electrical part and the outlet is ungrounded, there is a shock hazard. Free repair at (888) 402-6974, per the 2009 record.

**Sunbeam 07-562.** The firm is out of business, so the CPSC says to discard or destroy the unit.

**Thermador 01-155b** covers the self-clean door lock on combination thermal/microwave ovens, and **Microtronics 84-024** was a 1984 CPSC warning, because the maker had closed.

## Not a recall: notices and alerts people mistake for one

**Panasonic NN-SU158S quality improvement notice.** On February 9, 2026, [Panasonic](https://help.na.panasonic.com/answers/a-quality-improvement-notice-nnsu158s/) said the outer door panel of this 1.9 cu ft over-the-range model may loosen in some extreme cooking environments. It covers serials 6H79000001 to 6H13000001, made July 2019 to January 2023; the label is inside the front, with the door open.

Panasonic extended the door warranty to 5 years from purchase and gives 800-211-7262 (10 a.m. to 4 p.m. ET weekdays). Panasonic calls it a quality improvement notice, and our CPSC search by Panasonic returned no microwave recall.

**Toshiba microwaves starting on their own.** [Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org/product-safety/some-toshiba-microwaves-are-starting-on-their-own-a4659854545/) published a safety alert on June 25, 2026, based on 28 SaferProducts.gov reports. Midea, which makes Toshiba-brand microwaves, told CR the ML2-EC10SA power button can stick and trigger the 30-second add-time function, and that it found no evidence of a safety risk. It is a safety alert, not a recall: a CPSC search by Toshiba and Midea returned no recall on November 27, 2026. Details are on [Toshiba microwave turning on by itself](/guides/toshiba-microwave-turning-on-by-itself/).

## Recalled microwave accessories and containers

These recalls cover things you put in a microwave, not the oven. For the wider list, see [what not to put in a microwave](/guides/what-not-to-put-in-a-microwave/).

- **Whatever Works microwave griller K4001 (27-050, October 29, 2026):** about 8,709 units; can overheat and ignite; three fires. Full refund at 877-756-5053.
- **HSN Kitchen HQ insulated bowls (26-537, June 11, 2026):** about 86,040 units; metal lid springs can catch fire in the microwave. Refund from HSN.
- **LTD Commodities crisper pans (13-748):** the lid's metal washer can spark.
- **Illume "Love Today" mugs (16-112):** mislabeled microwave safe; the metallic print can spark.
- **Avon popcorn maker (13-193):** popcorn cooked too long can ignite.

## What to do if your microwave is recalled

- **Stop using it and unplug it**, as the CPSC remedy text says. For 04-577, cut power at the fuse or circuit breaker.
- **Call the number in the record** with the model and serial ready.
- **Expect the remedy the record names.** It may be a repair, a replacement or a rebate, depending on the recall.
- **Don't open the cabinet or repair it yourself.** GE's recall FAQ "strongly advises" against self-repair because of high-voltage parts in the control compartment; call service.
- **Don't use a microwave whose door doesn't close firmly** or is bent, warped or damaged, and stop using one that keeps running with the door open, per the [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/radiation-emitting-products/resources-you-radiation-emitting-products/microwave-ovens).

If it sparks or burns now, see [microwave fire: what to do](/guides/microwave-fire-what-to-do/).

## How to report a microwave problem that isn't a recall

File an incident report on SaferProducts.gov; the CR Toshiba alert was built on 28 of them. For a suspected radiation problem, the FDA says you may contact the manufacturer or complete and mail its Accidental Radiation Occurrence Report form. The federal limit, in 21 CFR 1030.10, is 5 mW/cm² at about 2 inches from the oven surface, over the oven's lifetime. More on that in [is microwave radiation safe](/guides/is-microwave-radiation-safe/).
