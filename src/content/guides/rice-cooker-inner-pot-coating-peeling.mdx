Rice cooker inner pot coating peeling is harmless according to Zojirushi and Panasonic, but not according to Xiaomi. Zojirushi's NL-BAC05 manual says peeling is "harmless to your health" and doesn't affect cooking, Keep Warm or sanitary properties, though it says to replace the pan if the peeling concerns you. Panasonic says it doesn't interfere with function or sanitation, only that rice sticks more. Xiaomi says further use is "not recommended" and not to eat flakes.

Per the manuals, a bent or deformed pot is the bigger problem. This page covers damage to the pot only. For the routine wash, see [how to clean a rice cooker](/guides/how-to-clean-a-rice-cooker/), and for a new cooker, the [best rice cookers](/best/rice-cookers/).

## Is a peeling rice cooker pot safe? What the manufacturers and the FDA say

**Zojirushi.** The [NL-BAC05 manual](https://store.zojirushi.com/cdn/shop/files/nl-bac_en.pdf) says the coating "may eventually discolor or peel off" and that this "will not affect the cooking / Keep Warm performances or its sanitary properties, and is harmless to your health." If the peeling concerns you, it says to replace the pan by buying a new one.

**Zojirushi FAQ.** Question 4 of Zojirushi's rice cooker FAQ says "all of our rice cookers' nonstick coated cooking pans are PFOA-free." The FAQ doesn't name the coating or mention peeling.

**Panasonic.** Its [support page on a peeling inner pan](https://help.na.panasonic.com/answers/troubleshooting-the-inside-of-the-inner-pan-has-started-to-peel/) says peeling "does not interfere with functionality or sanitation," but rice has a greater tendency to stick. If peelings are eaten with rice, it says they are "excreted without any absorption" and "none remains in the body." The page doesn't say whether to replace the pan.

**Xiaomi, the dissent.** Xiaomi's [support FAQ KA-12509](https://www.mi.com/uk/support/article/KA-12509) says a peeling pot is "not recommended for further use as it will affect the adhesion, do not consume any coating that comes off." It says the coating generally comes off because of scratches.

**FDA.** The FDA's page on [authorized uses of PFAS in food contact](https://www.fda.gov/food/process-contaminants-food/authorized-uses-pfas-food-contact-applications) (content current as of 01/03/2025) describes nonstick cookware as having "a highly polymerized coating bound to the surface." It says "negligible amounts of PFAS in this coating can migrate to food" and that polymerized PFAS "are not absorbed by the human body when ingested." That covers cookware coatings in general; the page doesn't mention rice cookers.

**Where evidence is thin.** Cuckoo's [CR-0655F manual](https://cuckooamerica.com/cdn/shop/files/CR-0655F_User_Manual.pdf) says its pot has a "non-stick fluorocarbon coating" but doesn't say whether peeling is harmful. Its one "harmless" line is about starch film, not the coating. Panasonic calls its coating a "fluorine coating," and the NL-BAC05 manual uses the same term once, but none of these makers names the specific compound for a given model.

## Why rice cooker coatings peel: causes listed in the manuals

- **Utensils and washing tools.** Whisks or a metal strainer when washing rice in the pan, metal ladles, hitting the pan with the spatula, and utensils or dishes placed inside (Zojirushi NL-BAC05 manual). Other cake pans or wax paper placed in the pan (NL-BAC05 cake troubleshooting). Sharp cutlery such as forks and spoons left in the pot while washing (Cuckoo CR-0655F manual).
- **Vinegar and seasonings.** Vinegar poured in for sushi rice, and seasonings not cleaned off right away (Zojirushi NL-BAC05 manual). Frequent vinegar, soy sauce or salty seasonings, and seasoned rice kept warm (Panasonic).
- **Cleaning.** The dishwasher or dish dryer, abrasive cleaners, nylon or metal brushes and melamine sponges (Zojirushi NL-BAC05 manual). A rough sponge, brush or abrasive, and tough or metallic scrubbers (Cuckoo CR-0655F manual). The abrasive side of a sponge and solutions with vinegar ([America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/articles/7279-how-to-clean-rice-cooker)).
- **Any scratch.** Panasonic says the coating "could peel if it is scratched regardless of how long the unit has been used," including from cutlery put in the pan while washing.
- **Stovetop or other uses.** Heating the inner pot on a gas stove or using it for other purposes can deform it or peel the coating, which may cause a malfunction (Cuckoo CR-0655F manual).
- **Age.** The coating "may wear out with use" (Zojirushi NL-BAC05) and "may wear away after long use" (Cuckoo CR-0655F).

Rice that sticks to bare spots can build up into a crust. For that, see [why a rice cooker burns rice on the bottom](/guides/rice-cooker-burning-rice-bottom/).

## Zojirushi inner pan replacement and other brands

**Zojirushi.** Question 1 of its rice cooker FAQ says to order inner pans by calling Customer Service at 800-733-6270, Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. PST, or through its online parts page.

The NL-BAC05 manual (its specifications cover the NL-BAC05 only) lists "Inner Cooking Pan B506-6B" in its Replacement Parts table, with Customer Service at 1-800-733-6270. It says to check the model number and part name first, then contact the store where you bought the cooker or Zojirushi Customer Service. Don't assume the same part fits the larger NL-BAC10.

The [NS-ZCC10/18 manual](https://store.zojirushi.com/cdn/shop/files/ns-zcc_en.pdf) text gives no inner-pan part number. Some replacement guides print one without a source, so confirm it with Zojirushi rather than ordering by a number you found online. {/* VERIFY: NS-ZCC10 inner pan part number, ask Zojirushi */}

**Cuckoo.** The CR-0655F manual says to contact the Customer Service center if the pot is dropped or deformed, and not to use a pot other than the one made for the product.

**Hamilton Beach.** The [37548 use and care guide](https://useandcares.hamiltonbeach.com/files/840264200.pdf) says a bent or warped pot "will not seat properly in base and, therefore, will not conduct heat correctly," and to call the toll-free Customer Assistance number to replace it. Its warranty excludes "wear from normal use."

**Across brands,** buy the pan made for your model. Zojirushi's NL-BAC05 manual says "Use the Inner Cooking Pan only for this Rice Cooker," and the NS-ZCC manual says not to use anything other than the specified inner pan because it may overheat and cause a malfunction. No prices are given here, since none was read on a maker's page for this guide.

## Rice cooker nonstick scratched? How to keep the coating from getting worse

From the Zojirushi NL-BAC05 manual unless noted:

1. Clean the pan only with soft implements such as a sponge. No nylon or metal brushes, melamine sponges or abrasive cleaners.
2. Clean the pan right after cooking anything with seasonings.
3. Don't pour vinegar into the pan, including for sushi rice; Panasonic and ATK also flag vinegar.
4. Don't use a metal ladle, and don't hit the pan with the spatula or other utensils.
5. Don't place utensils or dishes inside the pan, and don't wash it with cutlery inside (Cuckoo CR-0655F).
6. Don't wash rice with a whisk or a metal strainer in the pan.

The dishwasher is where brands conflict. Zojirushi's FAQ (question 5) says its rice cooker parts and accessories "are not dishwasher safe." The Hamilton Beach 37548 guide's Care and Cleaning page marks parts dishwasher-safe but says not to use the "SANI" setting, which could damage the product. Follow your own manual. For the full wash, see [how to clean a rice cooker](/guides/how-to-clean-a-rice-cooker/).

## Replace the pot or the whole cooker?

Replace only the pot if the coating is the only problem and the cooker otherwise works. Each maker above has a parts or service route.

Consider a new cooker if no pan is sold for your model, or if the cooker keeps failing to detect the pan. On the NS-ZCC10, H04 means the inner pan is not in place; see [Zojirushi rice cooker error codes](/guides/zojirushi-rice-cooker-error-codes/). For a replacement, start with the [best rice cookers](/best/rice-cookers/) and our research on the [Zojirushi NS-ZCC10](/reviews/zojirushi-ns-zcc10/) and the [Cuckoo CR-0655F](/reviews/cuckoo-cr-0655f/).

## Has any rice cooker been recalled for peeling coating?

Not that we found in the CPSC's records. A search of the CPSC recall database on Nov 18, 2026 for products named "cooker" returned 29 recalls, none about pot coatings. The one rice cooker recall among them, 17-017 for 3 Squares rice and slow cookers, was for improperly installed wiring that posed fire and electric shock hazards. Details are in the [rice cooker recall check](/guides/rice-cooker-recall-check/), and our method is on [how we research](/how-we-research/).
