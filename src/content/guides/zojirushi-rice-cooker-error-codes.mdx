Zojirushi error codes fall into two groups. Per Zojirushi's manuals, H codes (H01, H02, H04) are conditions you can usually clear yourself: the inside is too hot, or the inner pan isn't seated. E codes (E01, E02) mean a breakdown, and Zojirushi says to contact service. A blinking 7:00 means the clock's lithium battery is weak; the NL-BAC and NW-JEC manuals say the cooker functions normally once the time is set.

Codes vary by model, so match your model to the right row. Everything below comes from four of Zojirushi's own US instruction manuals, with the manual and page named on each row. Nothing here was tried on a cooker.

## Find your model first: Zojirushi codes aren't universal

Zojirushi publishes a separate manual for each model on its [instruction manuals page](https://store.zojirushi.com/pages/instruction-manual-type?type=rice-cookers), and its [rice cooker FAQ](https://store.zojirushi.com/pages/faq-rice-cookers) repeatedly tells owners to check theirs. The differences matter.

For the same "sensor too hot" condition, the NS-ZCC manual says just wait, the NL-BAC says open the lid more than 30 minutes, the NW-JEC more than 50 minutes, and the NS-WXC about 15 minutes. The NS-WXC manual's error table uses blinking lights instead of codes.

Third-party code lists don't settle it. A [Japanese repair article](https://lifestyle.assist-all.co.jp/zojirushi-rice-cooker-error-code-e/), written for owners in Japan, says E01/E02 point to a broken or poorly connected temperature sensor (thermistor) and E06 to a power-voltage or main-board problem. Its E06 matches the NW-JEC manual's voltage row, but no Zojirushi US manual we read names a failed part for E01/E02, and the article cites no Zojirushi source for it, so treat the part diagnosis as unconfirmed.

## H01 and H02: the inside is too hot

Per the [NS-ZCC10/18 manual](https://store.zojirushi.com/cdn/shop/files/ns-zcc_en.pdf) (p. 21), the cooker beeps 4 times when you press Cooking/Reheat, shows H01 or H02, and only the Reset key works. The cause is that the lid sensor or central sensor temperature is too high. The fix is to wait until the unit cools down, because it can't cook rice properly in that condition.

Steps by model:

1. NS-ZCC: only the Reset key works; wait until the unit cools. The manual gives no time.
2. [NL-BAC05/10](https://store.zojirushi.com/cdn/shop/files/nl-bac_en.pdf): press Keep Warm/Cancel and open the outer lid for more than 30 minutes.
3. [NW-JEC10/18](https://store.zojirushi.com/cdn/shop/files/nw-jec_en.pdf): press Cancel and open the outer lid for more than 50 minutes. To cool faster, both the NW-JEC and NL-BAC manuals say to fill the inner pan with cold water and set it in the body, or open the outer lid and remove the inner lid set so the radiator plate cools.

The NL-BAC and NW-JEC manuals add "be careful not to burn yourself." The error tables don't say what caused the overheat, but the NS-ZCC manual says to cook consecutive batches only after the body, outer lid and inner lid have cooled, and the NL-BAC and NW-JEC manuals ask for 30 and 50 minutes of cooling between batches.

## Zojirushi H04: the inner pan isn't detected

The NS-ZCC manual (p. 10) says pressing Cooking/Reheat before the inner pan is in the body "sounds the alarm" with four beeps and shows H04 for 5 seconds. The NW-JEC table (p. 36) lists H04 as "The Inner Cooking Pan is not inserted." To clear it:

1. Lift the pan out and wipe any water or residue off its outside and off the heating plate. The NS-ZCC troubleshooting chart lists objects on the heating plate or center sensor as a cause of cooking problems.
2. Set the pan back so it sits securely on the bottom of the main body.
3. Press Cooking/Reheat again.

Use only the pan that came with the cooker. The NS-ZCC manual says another pan "may cause the Inner Pan to overheat" and the cooker to malfunction. Its troubleshooting chart also lists a deformed inner pan as a cause; for worn or bent pans, see our [inner pot coating guide](/guides/rice-cooker-inner-pot-coating-peeling/). If H04 stays with the right pan seated, the NS-ZCC troubleshooting guide's catch-all says to contact an authorized service center or Zojirushi customer service, and the NW-JEC table says to contact the store or Zojirushi.

## Zojirushi E01 error and E02: a breakdown, not a reset

The NS-ZCC manual (p. 21) is direct: if E01 or E02 shows and the keys don't work, "This is a breakdown." Contact an authorized service center or Zojirushi customer service. The NL-BAC and NW-JEC troubleshooting pages both ask "Does the Display show E01 or E02?" and point to an error table whose malfunction row says to contact the store or Zojirushi. On the NW-JEC, that row also lists E07, E13 and E21.

No manual we read gives a home fix or names the failed part. Don't open the unit; call 800-733-6270, Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. PST, per Zojirushi's FAQ.

## E06, E17 and H17 on the NW-JEC pressure model

The NW-JEC manual (p. 36) has two rows the other manuals don't. E06 is a voltage abnormality that makes the unit stop itself "to prevent a breakdown": check that the outlet's rated voltage matches the cooker, and use another outlet if not. E17 or H17 means the outer lid isn't completely closed: press Cancel, close the lid fully, press Start/Reheat, and contact the store or Zojirushi if that doesn't work. The manual also notes the error buzzer sounds regardless of the sound setting.

## Zojirushi rice cooker blinking: clock, lights and keep-warm hours

### Blinking 7:00 or clock

A lithium battery keeps the clock and timer memory while the cooker is unplugged. Per the NS-ZCC manual, it lasts about 4 to 5 years at 68 °F with the unit unplugged. When it weakens, the clock shows 7:00 and keeps blinking on plug-in; reset the time and it works normally, but the display clears when unplugged.

The manual says to contact service and "do not attempt to replace the lithium battery by yourself." The NL-BAC and NW-JEC error tables also show a blinking 7:00 for a dead battery; NL-BAC says Zojirushi replaces it "with additional charge" and NW-JEC says "a fee will be charged." The NL-BAC timer won't accept a setting until the time is set.

### iFixit's DIY battery guide

An [iFixit community guide](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Zojirushi+Rice+Cooker+Model+NS-LAC05++Battery+Replacement/147771) replaces the NS-LAC05's CR2450 coin cell, which is soldered to the board. It rates the job moderate, about 1 hour, with a soldering iron, and warns "Don't remove the bottom board" because the buttons were hard to reseat. The NS-LAC05 isn't one of the four manuals we read, but the NS-ZCC manual says "do not attempt to replace the lithium battery by yourself," so the guide cuts against Zojirushi's instructions.

### Blinking lights on the NS-WXC

The [NS-WXC10/18 manual](https://store.zojirushi.com/cdn/shop/files/ns-wxc_en.pdf) (p. 18): all lights blinking together is a malfunction, so contact the store or Zojirushi. Both lights blinking together means a sensor is too hot: press Reset and open the lid about 15 minutes. If COOKING and KEEP WARM blink together after a power failure, discard the rice and restart.

### Blinking keep-warm hours

On the NL-BAC, the elapsed-hours number blinks after 12 hours as a warning to eat the rice soon. It blinks at "0(h)" on menus that shouldn't be kept warm, or when the rice and pan are already cool. For food safety, see [how long rice can stay in a rice cooker](/guides/how-long-can-rice-stay-in-rice-cooker/).

## Power failures and an odd or frozen display

Per the NS-ZCC manual, a power cut under 10 minutes during cooking is bridged by the battery memory and cooking continues; over 10 minutes, the cooker returns to Reset mode. If the display looks wrong or keys don't respond, unplug and replug it; 7:00 blinks, and you reset the timer and time. The NL-BAC gives the same odd-display instruction. Clattering noises during cooking are the microcomputer adjusting heat, "not a malfunction," per the NS-ZCC manual.

## When to stop troubleshooting and call Zojirushi

- Any E01 or E02, or all lights blinking on an NS-WXC.
- An H code that returns after the cooker has fully cooled and the pan is seated.
- Steam leaking between the outer lid and body: the NS-ZCC manual blames a deformed inner lid or cut gasket, replaced through customer service.
- A clock battery replacement.

Use the phone number above, the e-mail link or the service-center list on Zojirushi's FAQ. An error code is not a recall; to check recalls, see our [rice cooker recall check](/guides/rice-cooker-recall-check/).
