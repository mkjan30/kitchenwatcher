## Food safety

Chicken is safe at 165°F, per the [USDA FSIS safe minimum internal temperature chart](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart). Probe the thickest thigh at its thickest part and keep the tip off the bone, which reads hotter than the meat. Thighs have enough fat that a few degrees past 165°F doesn't dry them out.

## Tips

- Dry skin crisps; wet skin steams. Pat the thighs dry before the oil goes on.
- Start skin side down so the fat under the skin renders, then finish skin side up to crisp it.
- Watch the skin where it rests on the basket. {/* MEASURE: skin result, crisp all over or soft spots where thighs touched, describe */}
- The Vortex Plus may prompt you to turn the food partway through. {/* MEASURE: whether a Turn Food prompt appears in Air Fry mode, and at what minute */}
