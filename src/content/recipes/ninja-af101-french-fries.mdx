## Why this model

The AF101 is Ninja's 4-quart basket fryer (manufacturer spec), the small one. Fries are where a small basket shows: pile them up and the middle steams. My full test is in the [Ninja AF101 review](/reviews/ninja-af101/), it's ranked on [the best air fryers page](/best/air-fryers/), and if you're choosing between it and the 5-quart model, see [Ninja AF101 vs AF141](/vs/ninja-af101-vs-ninja-af141/).

## Soak, dry, oil

Ninja's own hand-cut fries recipe, written for the 5-quart AF141, soaks 1 pound of russets in cold water for 30 minutes, dries them, and uses anywhere from half a tablespoon to 3 tablespoons of oil, with more oil giving crisper fries. [Air Fryer Fanatics](https://airfryerfanatics.com/air-fryer-french-fries-from-fresh/) skips the soak, cooks at 400°F for 10 to 12 minutes in batches, and notes you must dry the potatoes completely before the oil goes on.

## Tips

- Cut evenly. Thin ends burn before thick sticks cook through.
- Salt right away, while the surface is still oily, so it sticks.
- Eat them fast. {/* MEASURE: crisp at 10 minutes out of the basket, still crisp or limp, describe */}
- Oil and starch bake onto the crisper plate. {/* MEASURE: oil left in the basket after cooking, describe */} My [AF101 cleaning guide](/guides/how-to-clean-ninja-af101/) covers getting it off without wrecking the coating.
