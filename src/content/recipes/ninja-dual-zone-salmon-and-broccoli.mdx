## Why this model

The DZ201 is an 8-quart machine split into two independent baskets with separate controls (manufacturer spec). Smart Finish is the reason to cook this in it rather than a single-basket fryer: you set two different times and temperatures and the unit handles the delay. My full test of the machine is in the [Ninja DZ201 review](/reviews/ninja-dz201/), and it's ranked against the others on [the best air fryers page](/best/air-fryers/).

## Food safety

Fish is safe at 145°F, per the [USDA FSIS safe minimum internal temperature chart](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart), or when the flesh turns opaque and separates easily with a fork. Probe the thickest fillet in its center.

## Tips

- Cut the broccoli to one size. Mixed sizes leave small florets burnt and big stems raw. {/* MEASURE: broccoli doneness difference between top and bottom of the pile, describe */}
- Skin side down on the crisper plate. {/* MEASURE: salmon skin stuck to the crisper plate or released clean, describe */}
- Shake the broccoli once. {/* MEASURE: does pulling basket 2 to shake pause zone 1, yes or no */}
- For four people, the salmon needs its own basket and so does the broccoli, so check the fit before you double the recipe (see the table above).
