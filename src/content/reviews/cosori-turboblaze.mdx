The Cosori TurboBlaze (model CAF-DC601) is a 6-quart, single-drawer air fryer with a DC fan motor and a 450°F top setting, about $90 at COSORI.com on Sep 26, 2026. {/* MEASURE: verdict — one sentence, who it's for, and the number that decided it (e.g. grams of fries per layer or dB at 1 m) */} It suits two to four people who want a square basket and a quieter fan.

Three questions decide this one: how much food fits in one layer, how close the basket gets to 450°F, and how loud the fan is. Any number I attribute to another site is theirs; the method for every test is on [how we test](/how-we-research/). {/* MEASURE: one line on how long we used it and how many cooks, days and count */}

## Specs and price

Per COSORI's product page (manufacturer spec):

- **Capacity:** 6 quarts (5.7 L), square basket
- **Power:** 1,725 W, 120 V
- **Temperature range:** 90–450°F
- **Timer:** 1 minute to 24 hours
- **Fan:** DC motor, 5 speeds, tied to the cooking function (you can't pick the speed)
- **Functions:** Air Fry, Roast, Bake, Broil, Dehydrate, Frozen, Proof, Reheat, Keep Warm
- **Size and weight:** 11.8 x 14.4 x 11.9 in with handle, 13.2 lb
- **Coating:** PFAS-free ceramic on the basket and crisper plate, both dishwasher safe

COSORI's launch release claims it cooks 1 lb of fresh fries up to 46% faster than its own older CP168 model ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cosori-unveils-the-next-generation-6-quart-turboblaze-air-fryer-301961219.html)). That's the maker comparing against itself, so I don't lean on it.

Price: about $90 at COSORI.com on Sep 26, 2026, down from a $119.99 list price. {/* MEASURE: price paid and retailer on purchase day, USD */}

## Noise

The DC motor is the TurboBlaze's main selling point after capacity. Home Appliance Owl lists it at under 53 dB and puts typical Ninja and Instant models at 60–65 dB ([Home Appliance Owl](https://www.homeapplianceowl.com/air-fryers/cosori-turboblaze-review/)); I couldn't find that figure on COSORI's own page. {/* MEASURE: dB(A) at 1 m in air fry at 400°F, and room background dB for reference */} Side by side with the Ninja, that number is in [TurboBlaze vs Ninja AF141](/vs/cosori-turboblaze-vs-ninja-af141/).

## What owners report

- **Inconsistent results on fries and vegetables.** TechRadar's review headline says so ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/home/small-appliances/cosori-turboblaze-6l-air-fryer-review-a-convenient-multifunctional-air-fryer-with-inconsistent-results)), and Home Appliance Owl collects the same complaint ([Home Appliance Owl](https://www.homeapplianceowl.com/air-fryers/cosori-turboblaze-review/)).
- **Small food falls through the crisper plate** center hole ([TechGearLab](https://www.techgearlab.com/reviews/kitchen/air-fryer/cosori-turboblaze)).
- **Drawer fit.** Some buyers report trouble seating the drawer, and the rear cable tidy keeps it off the wall ([Home Appliance Owl](https://www.homeapplianceowl.com/air-fryers/cosori-turboblaze-review/)).
