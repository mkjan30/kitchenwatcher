The Cuckoo CR-0655F is a 6-cup micom rice cooker that reviewers praise for long-grain and basmati rice and for its adjustable soak and heat settings. The experts split on it. Engadget named it the best rice cooker for most people (updated Jan 2, 2026). America's Test Kitchen rated it Recommended with Reservations (Oct 23, 2025) because short-grain and brown rice came out a little wet at Cuckoo's water ratios.

It ranks #2 on our [best rice cookers](/best/rice-cookers/) list. This Cuckoo CR-0655F review compiles published testing and the manufacturer's manual; we do not cook with the machine ([how we research](/how-we-research/)).

## What do reviewers say about the Cuckoo CR-0655F?

### America's Test Kitchen: Recommended with Reservations

[America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/equipment-reviews/2328-rice-cookers) got "great results" with long-grain white rice. The problem was the other grains: short-grain white and brown rice "were a little wet" at Cuckoo's recommended ratios. Its testers also found the water lines, imprinted into the metal pot, hard to read. They called the display buttons "hard to decipher" because labels sit both above and below them. The cooker gives five loud beeps before it switches to keep warm.

ATK cooked 50 batches across its roundup, including small and large long-grain batches, half-capacity short-grain with a 2-hour keep-warm hold, medium-grain brown rice, and a temperature data logger. Its 12-hour keep-warm and quick-cook tests covered only its top three models, which did not include this Cuckoo.

### Engadget: best rice cooker for most people

[Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/home/kitchen-tech/best-rice-cooker-120015478.html) said the CR-0655F delivered "some of the best results for all three grain types" its tester cooked, and did particularly well with long-grain basmati. Engadget's tester liked the white rice most after half an hour on warm and would have liked a slightly longer cook on the brown rice.

Engadget also listed "unexpected design flaws" as its con. The lid "springs open with enough force to rock the whole appliance", so Engadget recommends an oven mitt against the steam, and its tester saw that only with Cuckoo machines. The water-level markings are recessed into the pot, so they need a closer eye in cleaning to keep starchy film from getting trapped.

### Serious Eats: best Cuckoo rice cooker

[Serious Eats](https://www.seriouseats.com/the-best-rice-cookers) (updated Oct 14, 2026) named it the best Cuckoo rice cooker. It says the cooker suits buyers who want more control than texture presets give, since you can set how long the rice soaks and how hot the cooker gets. Its reviewers report "exceptional" jasmine rice. Its one stated drawback: beginners may find the presets and customization options overwhelming.

### Where the reviewers disagree

Engadget reported "some of the best results for all three grain types" it cooked, though it wanted a slightly longer brown-rice cook. ATK found short-grain and brown rice a little wet at Cuckoo's ratios. All three reviewers are positive on long-grain rice (ATK long-grain white, Engadget basmati, Serious Eats jasmine), so that is the best-supported use case. For short-grain or brown rice, expect to adjust the water; our [water ratio guide](/guides/rice-cooker-water-ratio/) covers the general rules.

## How much rice the CR-0655F holds, and how long it cooks

Capacity depends on the menu. Per the manual, the Glutinous menu ("when you cook white rice") holds 2 to 6 cups. Turbo, Mixed/Brown and GABA hold 2 to 4 cups, and Porridge holds 1 to 1.5 cups. The manual's menu table sends "long rice or brown rice" to the Mixed/Brown menu, which tops out at 4 cups, so the full 6 cups applies only on the Glutinous menu. See [rice cooker cup size](/guides/rice-cooker-cup-size/) for what a cooker cup measures.

Manufacturer cook times by menu: Glutinous 35–53 min, Turbo 32–44 min, Mixed/Brown 55–68 min, GABA 83–91 min, Porridge 90 min and Auto Clean about 25 min. The manual's Turbo section separately says about 31 to 36 minutes, so the manual disagrees with itself on Turbo. It suggests soaking the rice about 20 minutes before a Turbo cook and says Turbo cannot be preset.

The manual explains a common complaint too. It says rice can be light yellow at the bottom because the cooker "is designed to improve pleasant flavor". The manual calls this "melanoizing", says it is not a malfunction, and says it is stronger with preset cooking. For general brown-rice advice, see [brown rice in a rice cooker](/guides/brown-rice-in-a-rice-cooker/); for crust that goes past light yellow, see [rice cooker burning rice on the bottom](/guides/rice-cooker-burning-rice-bottom/).

## Keep-warm and reheat limits in the CR-0655F manual

The manual recommends keeping rice warm for less than 12 hours, because longer holds can discolor the rice or develop an odor. The elapsed-time display blinks after 24 hours. It advises against keeping mixed or brown rice warm "if at all possible" and against leaving the rice scoop in the pot. Reheat takes about 9 minutes, and the manual says to use it once or twice a day.

Keep warm draws 80 W against 580 W for cooking, per the manual. The manual's reason for the 12-hour figure is quality (discoloration and odor), and it gives no safe holding time. For federal food-safety guidance on holding and reheating cooked rice, read [how long rice can stay in a rice cooker](/guides/how-long-can-rice-stay-in-rice-cooker/).

## Using My Mode, Turbo and GABA

My Mode lets you set the soak and heat levels for the Glutinous, GABA and Mixed/Brown menus. The manual warns that it "may influence scorching" and can lengthen the cook, and that soak and heat cannot be readjusted during cooking, warming or a preset. Engadget said the presets work well for cooks who don't want to bother with My Mode, and liked having the option.

GABA is a germinated-brown-rice menu. Per the manual, brown rice soaks in water to pre-germinate for no more than 16 hours, and the GABA time is set to 0, 2, 4 or 6 hours. Turbo is the fast white-rice option covered above.

## Cleaning the CR-0655F and its inner pot

To run Auto Clean, the manual says to fill the pot with water to scale 2, lock the lid, choose Auto Clean from Menu (or press Timer/Auto clean twice), then press Cook/Turbo. The cycle takes about 25 minutes. Engadget notes that the recessed water lines need extra attention so starch does not collect in them.

The manual describes the pot as having a "non-stick fluorocarbon coating" and warns that the coating "may wear away after long use". It rules out metal or abrasive scrubbers and sharp cutlery in the pot, and says to wash the pot and detachable cover with neutral detergent and dry them.

The English cleaning instructions call for dishwashing detergent and a soft cloth or sponge and do not mention a dishwasher either way. For general steps see [how to clean a rice cooker](/guides/how-to-clean-a-rice-cooker/), and for worn coatings see [inner pot coating peeling](/guides/rice-cooker-inner-pot-coating-peeling/).

## Error codes in the CR-0655F manual

The manual lists five error conditions:

- the inner pot is not detected or is misplaced
- a temperature sensor issue
- the center sensor or heater causing abnormally long cooking
- a preset set longer than 13 hours
- a MICOM inner memory issue

Cuckoo America's [error-codes page](https://cuckooamerica.com/pages/error-codes) names the CR-0655F for the inner-pot and over-13-hour errors. Its rows for temperature sensor, center sensor and Micom memory problems say to call customer service and list no model.

Both the manual and Cuckoo's page show the code letters as images, so we have not listed them yet.

## Who should buy the CR-0655F and who should skip it

Buy it if you mostly cook long-grain white, basmati or jasmine rice and want soak and heat control for about $140. ATK, Engadget and Serious Eats all reported good long-grain results, and My Mode gives you room to adjust.

Skip it if you mostly cook short-grain or brown rice and want it right at stock ratios; ATK found both a little wet. Skip it too if a lid that springs open hard bothers you (Engadget), or if you want a simple machine; Serious Eats says beginners may find the options overwhelming. For short-grain rice, the Zojirushi NS-ZCC10 is the alternative on our hub.

The 6-cup rating applies to the Glutinous (white) menu; Mixed/Brown, GABA and Turbo stop at 4 cups. Not sure that is enough? See [what size rice cooker you need](/guides/what-size-rice-cooker-do-i-need/).
