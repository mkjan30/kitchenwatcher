Skip it if noise or smell bothers you. Food Network puts the fan at up to 66 dB, and the new-unit plastic smell is owners' top complaint.

## What lab tests found: accurate heat, slower preheat

**Heat accuracy is the standout.** TechGearLab measured an average of 401.5°F at a 400°F setting and gave it 10 of 10 for temperature accuracy ([TechGearLab](https://www.techgearlab.com/reviews/kitchen/air-fryer/instant-pot-vortex-plus)). Across TechGearLab's comparison, that beats the Ninja Foodi 2-basket's 386.4°F at the same setting ([TechGearLab best air fryers](https://www.techgearlab.com/topics/kitchen/best-air-fryer)).

**Preheat is slower than rivals'.** TechGearLab measured a 3-minute preheat and lists a longer preheat than competitors as a con.

**Results are good, not the crispiest everywhere.** Food Network calls it Best Overall after comparing more than 25 models and credits it with the crispiest, most even results across fries, chicken and cake ([Food Network](https://www.foodnetwork.com/how-to/packages/shopping/product-reviews/best-air-fryer)). TechGearLab disagrees in part: it scored cooking quality 7 of 10 and found crispiness underwhelming on some foods. Overall it ranked the Vortex Plus third of ten, with a score of 78. Reviewed found the single-basket 6-quart crispy and even ([Reviewed](https://www.reviewed.com/cooking/content/instant-vortex-plus-air-fryers-review)).

**Size.** TechGearLab measured 87.8 square inches of cooking area and calls the unit bigger than competitors. The Ninja AF101 has 53.5 square inches by the same measure, which is why that one suits one or two people and this one suits more ([Ninja AF101 review](/reviews/ninja-af101/)).

**Noise.** Food Network lists the fan as loud at up to 66 decibels. No other source I found published a decibel figure.

## Does the Vortex Plus plastic smell go away?

The plastic smell is the complaint that comes up most. Smell is the top complaint in Best Buy's owner reviews (8 mentions out of 113 reviews), and owners describe the burn-off period as long. TechGearLab went further: fries and tots kept a plastic flavor no matter how often the unit was cleaned and preheated, and the review says the taste persisted even after months.

The OdorErase filter on some versions doesn't solve cooking smells either. Reviewed noticed little difference with it, and bacon and fried chicken odors still filled the kitchen ([Reviewed](https://www.reviewed.com/cooking/content/instant-vortex-plus-air-fryers-review)).

So the evidence splits. Most owners say the smell fades, while one lab says the taste never fully left. If you're sensitive to it, buy from a store with an easy return policy.

## Vortex Plus problems: E1 and E2 codes, recalls

- **E1 and E2 codes.** Instant's user manual for the 140-3006-01 (the May 2024 revision linked from its product page) lists two error codes: E1, an open circuit, and E2, a temperature sensor short circuit. For both, the fix is to contact Instant's Consumer Care ([user manual](https://embed.widencdn.net/download/worldkitchen/h3ykivbmnv/140-3006-01_IB_VortexPlusAF_6Qt_SS_US_Manual_R10-FINAL-05-21-2024_LoRes.pdf?u=twfrp7)).
- **Tray slides out when you tip the basket**, per TechGearLab's cons.
- **Thin manual.** Reviewed found little cooking guidance for common foods.
- **Clearance.** Best Buy owners mention leaving 5 to 6 inches from the wall.
- **Recalls.** I found no CPSC recall for the Vortex Plus 6 quart as of Sep 27, 2026 ([CPSC](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls)).

Nobody I found has published a watt-draw figure or a multi-year durability record for this model, so I can't tell you how the coating holds up past the first year.

## Who the Vortex Plus 6 quart suits (and who should skip it)

**Buy it** if you cook for three or four, want a basket that holds its set temperature, and like a simple dial. At the $90 Food Network showed, that accuracy is good value; at the $150 list price, compare first.

**Skip it** if you cook for one or two, since the [Ninja AF101](/reviews/ninja-af101/) takes far less counter space. Skip it too if you want a main and a side done at once, which the two-basket [Ninja DZ201](/reviews/ninja-dz201/) handles. If the plastic taste reports worry you, compare the [Cosori TurboBlaze](/reviews/cosori-turboblaze/), another 6-quart basket.
