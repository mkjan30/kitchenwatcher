The Instant Vortex Plus 6 quart is a 1700-watt, single-basket stainless air fryer with six cooking functions and a 95 to 400°F range, per Instant's product page. {/* MEASURE: one-sentence verdict from our results — preheat seconds, fries minutes to golden, noise dB — and who it suits */} This review answers two questions: does it earn its counter space against the other 6-quart baskets on our list, and does the plastic smell owners complain about go away.

{/* MEASURE: photo — unit on counter with a 12-inch ruler for scale, basket closed */}

## Specs and price

Instant lists the stainless Vortex Plus 6 quart (model 140-3006-01) at 1700 W, 14.92 by 12.36 by 12.83 inches, with air fry, bake, roast, broil, dehydrate and reheat, a dishwasher-safe nonstick basket and tray, and a one-year limited warranty. Instant also says it needs "little to no preheating," which I test below.

Instant sells more than one Vortex Plus 6 quart. Some versions add a ClearCook window and OdorErase filters; the stainless 140-3006-01 page does not list them. {/* MEASURE: photo — model label on the underside of our unit */}

## How this review is tested

Every air fryer on this site goes through the same run so the numbers line up across reviews ([full method](/how-we-test/)):

- **Heat:** a probe thermometer clipped at basket center logs the climb from a cold start to 400°F, then the average swing around the set point for 10 minutes. The display saying "ready" does not count.
- **Food:** one even layer of fresh-cut fries and one layer of wings, weighed and counted, cooked at 400°F until golden or 175°F internal. Browning is scored piece by piece, not by eye across the pile.
- **Noise:** a sound meter 1 m away at basket height, with the kitchen's background level noted so it can be subtracted.
- **Power and heat:** a plug-in watt meter for the whole cycle, and an IR thermometer on the housing, handle and window after 20 minutes at 400°F.
- **Cleanup:** a stopwatch from last bite to dry basket, and photos of the coating at the start and end.

Spec-sheet numbers stay in the "Manufacturer spec" column. Nothing moves into "Our measurement" until it has been measured.

## What owners report

- **Plastic smell when new.** The top complaint in Best Buy owner reviews, and TechGearLab tasted plastic in fries ([TechGearLab](https://www.techgearlab.com/reviews/kitchen/air-fryer/instant-pot-vortex-plus)).
- **E1 and E2 errors.** The Vortex Plus manual lists E1 as an open-circuit temperature sensor and E2 as a shorted sensor, and tells owners to contact Instant's customer care ([ManualsLib](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/2601994/Instant-Vortex-Plus.html?page=27)).
- **Noise and footprint.** Best Buy reviewers mention both.
- **Recalls.** I found no CPSC recall for the Vortex Plus 6 quart when I searched on Sep 26, 2026 ([CPSC recalls](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls)).

## Before you buy

- **Which Vortex Plus.** Check the model number on the box. The stainless 140-3006-01 is the one this review covers; a ClearCook version with a window and odor filters also sells as a 6-quart Vortex Plus.
- **Counter depth.** At 14.92 inches long per Instant, it sticks out further than the 4-quart [Ninja AF101](/reviews/ninja-af101/). {/* MEASURE: depth including the basket handle, inches */}
- **First run.** Owners suggest running it empty before the first cook to burn off the smell; the test above logs how long that takes.
