This Moccamaster KBT review rests on two lab reviews that both rank it first. The KBT is a 10-cup drip brewer with a thermal carafe, a manual drip-stop and no timer, $349 at Moccamaster's US store on Oct 4, 2026. [America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/equipment_reviews/2461-best-coffee-makers-automatic-drip) named it the winner, with brew water in the ideal range for 91% of the cycle. [TechGearLab](https://www.techgearlab.com/reviews/kitchen/drip-coffee-maker/technivorm-moccamaster-kbt) ranked it #1 of 11. Skip it if you want coffee ready on a timer.

It ranks fourth in our [best coffee makers](/best/coffee-makers/) hub, where price counts against it. Below is every number I could source, labeled by who produced it, plus the places where the sources disagree.

## Moccamaster KBT review: quick verdict

**Buy it if:**

- You want steady brew temperature: ATK found 91% of the cycle in its ideal range (its Breville Precision Brewer logged 94%), and TechGearLab measured a 3.36°F standard deviation across the pots it brewed.
- You keep machines a long time. Moccamaster's manual gives a new brewer 5 years of warranty, and ATK says the plastic parts are easy and cheap to replace.
- You like controlling the drip yourself, which the manual drip-stop allows (ATK).

**Skip it if:**

- You want a timer. CNET, ATK and TechGearLab all say it has none.
- You want coffee hot for most of the day. The hold-time claims run from 1 to 6 hours, and only one is backed by a stated method.
- You are buying for SCA certification. The KBT is not on the SCA's list.

## Moccamaster KBT brew temperature: claimed vs measured

Moccamaster claims a brew temperature of 196-205°F. TechGearLab measured an average peak of 201.7°F, inside that range. Its average across the entire brew was 193°F, which sits below both Moccamaster's claim and the 195-205°F range TechGearLab calls ideal.

ATK measured the share of time rather than an average: the brew water stayed in ATK's ideal zone of 194-205°F for 91% of the cycle. ATK credits the copper heating element for heating fast and holding steady. CNET cites the SCA for an ideal window of 197-205°F but prints no KBT reading.

Altitude matters too. The [manual](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0528/3545/1043/files/v23.0_ENG_MM_IM_web.pdf?v=1621537159) says the element brings water to a boil, and the boiling point can fall to 198°F at higher elevations, so brewing runs cooler there. For what the temperature standard means, see [what an SCA-certified coffee maker is](/guides/what-is-sca-certified-coffee-maker/).

## How long the Technivorm KBT thermal carafe keeps coffee hot

Short answer: the sources disagree, from 1 hour to 6. Moccamaster's KBT page says the glass-lined stainless carafe keeps coffee quality "for up to an hour." ATK, which checks carafe temperature fresh, at 1 hour and at 2 hours, says it kept coffee piping hot for 2 hours, but it publishes no temperatures for those checkpoints.

[CNET](https://www.cnet.com/home/kitchen-and-household/best-coffee-maker/) lists "Keeps coffee hot for 6 hours" as a pro. Its own temperature table stops at 60 minutes and has no Moccamaster row, so that figure has no published measurement. TechGearLab measured 177°F in the carafe, averaged over five brew cycles, but does not say how long after brewing. A photo caption says the carafe keeps coffee hot "for hours," with no time measured.

Moccamaster's manual claims the brewer holds coffee at 175-185°F. Moccamaster's page says every thermal carafe comes with a Brew-Thru Lid and a Travel Lid. The manual says to use the travel lid for transport, and TechGearLab says the travel lid seals the carafe completely and keeps coffee hot longer.

The manual also warns: no dairy or baby food in the carafe, no carbonated drinks, do not drink straight from it, and it is not dishwasher safe. For how thermal carafes compare with hot plates in general, see [thermal vs glass carafe](/guides/thermal-vs-glass-carafe/).

## The 2025 thermal carafe redesign

ATK reports that Technivorm redesigned the KBT's thermal carafe in 2025. Per ATK, the new carafe also kept coffee piping hot for 2 hours and has an improved pour spout. No other source I read describes the change, so details beyond the spout are unknown. I can't say which carafe a given retailer is shipping.

## Using it: drip-stop, half pots, filters and ratio

The manual recommends brewing no less than 4 cups (16 oz) in its 8- to 10-cup brewers. For a smaller batch, ATK says the manual drip-stop lets you slow the drip from the basket so water spends enough time in the smaller bed of grounds. It can also hold water back to preinfuse.

The manual's ratio is 55 g of coffee per liter, or 2 tablespoons per 6 oz. For the 40 oz KBT that is 7 level scoops, or 69 g. It calls for a medium to medium-coarse grind and oxygen-whitened paper filters, and advises against gold-mesh or other reusable filters. It also says not to use reverse osmosis or distilled water, which can damage the boiling element. More on dosing in [coffee-to-water ratio for drip](/guides/coffee-to-water-ratio-drip/).

[The Way to Coffee](https://www.thewaytocoffee.com/moccamaster-kbgt-review/) calls the KBGT basically the same machine, with an automatic drip-stop that closes when you lift the carafe. If you are choosing between thermal and glass, see [Moccamaster KBT vs KBGV Select](/vs/moccamaster-kbt-vs-moccamaster-kbgv-select/).

## Descaling, cleaning and warranty fine print

The manual says to descale every 100 cycles or at least every 3 months, and it does not recommend vinegar. TechGearLab timed a descale at 18 minutes and notes the brewer has no descale indicator. Steps are in [how to descale a coffee maker](/guides/how-to-descale-a-coffee-maker/), and the vinegar question in [vinegar vs descaler](/guides/vinegar-vs-descaler-coffee-maker/).

The 5-year warranty covers the brewer "excluding carafe and parts." A refurbished brewer gets 1 year. Per the manual, the warranty does not cover defects from lack of descaling ("you must descale... to maintain performance and warranty coverage"). Service by anyone other than an authorized service center, or removing the bottom panel, also voids it.

Hand-wash the carafe and basket after each use. For oil stains, the manual says to soak the thermal carafe overnight with a little dishwasher detergent and water.

## What KBT owners complain about: price, no timer, plastics

- **Overall sentiment.** [RedditRecs](https://redditrecs.com/drip-coffee-maker/model/moccamaster-technivorm-kbt/) counts Reddit owners at 85% positive: 88 positive, 6 negative, 9 neutral.
- **Top cons on Reddit**, per RedditRecs: no programmable timer, high price, fragile plastic components and uneven water distribution over the grounds. One commenter says it has "plastics that won't survive heavyhanded use." RedditRecs gives no per-complaint counts.
- **Top praise** is durability and readily available parts. One cafe worker quoted by RedditRecs calls it bomb-proof, brewing a full batch every few hours from 5am to 9pm.
- **CNET's cons:** pricier, takes up a lot of space, not programmable.

## Is the Moccamaster KBT SCA certified?

Not by the SCA's own list. Moccamaster's KBT page says it is "ECBC certified and SCA approved," the manual says it meets SCA Golden Cup brewing standards, ATK's spec list says "SCA Certified: Yes," and TechGearLab lists it as an SCA Certified Home Brewer, adding that "you'll see a lot of Moccamasters on the list."

The [SCA's Certified Home Equipment page](https://sca.coffee/certified-home-brewer), checked on October 4, 2026, lists brewers from OXO, Fellow, Breville, KRUPS, Zwilling and others, but no Technivorm or Moccamaster model. The SCA says certification runs on a three-year licensing term, and I could not confirm whether any Moccamaster was listed earlier. I don't call it certified until the SCA lists it.

## Is Moccamaster worth it? Who should skip the KBT

On the evidence, yes, if brew quality and longevity are what you pay for. It tops both lab reviews with numbers behind them, it carries a 5-year warranty, and Reddit owners mostly praise durability and parts support. Against that: $349 at Moccamaster's US store on Oct 4, 2026, no timer, a carafe the warranty does not cover, hold-time claims that conflict, and an SCA claim the SCA does not list.

**Skip it** if price or a timer matters more. The [OXO Brew 8-Cup](/reviews/oxo-brew-8-cup/) is on the SCA's list, though ATK measured it in the ideal range for 54% of the cycle against the KBT's 91%. The head-to-head is in [OXO Brew 8-Cup vs Moccamaster KBT](/vs/oxo-brew-8-cup-vs-moccamaster-kbt/). If you want coffee ready when you wake up, the [Ninja CE251](/reviews/ninja-ce251/) has scheduled brewing, per TechGearLab.
