The Ninja AF101 is a 1550-watt, 4-quart single-basket air fryer with four functions (air crisp, roast, reheat, dehydrate) and a 105 to 400°F range, per its Amazon listing. {/* MEASURE: one-sentence verdict from our results — preheat seconds, fries grams per layer and minutes to golden, noise dB — and who it suits */} It is sized for one or two people, and the question owners ask most is how long its ceramic basket coating lasts.

{/* MEASURE: photo — unit on counter with ruler for scale */}

## Specs and price

Per the Amazon listing: 4 quarts, 1550 W, 105 to 400°F, dishwasher-safe parts. The basket and crisper plate are ceramic-coated. The AF101 is the same unit as the AF100 with a multi-layer rack and a longer recipe booklet added, according to retailer Q&A. Consumer Reports lists it at 13 by 11 by 14 inches and 10 lb, from $119.99 ([Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org/appliances/air-fryers/ninja-af101/m399741/)).

## How this review is tested

Every air fryer on this site goes through the same run so the numbers line up across reviews ([full method](/how-we-test/)):

- **Heat:** a probe thermometer clipped at basket center logs the climb from a cold start to 400°F, then the average swing around the set point for 10 minutes. The display saying "ready" does not count.
- **Food:** one even layer of fresh-cut fries and one layer of wings, weighed and counted, cooked at 400°F until golden or 175°F internal. Browning is scored piece by piece, not by eye across the pile.
- **Noise:** a sound meter 1 m away at basket height, with the kitchen's background level noted so it can be subtracted.
- **Power and heat:** a plug-in watt meter for the whole cycle, and an IR thermometer on the housing, handle and window after 20 minutes at 400°F.
- **Cleanup:** a stopwatch from last bite to dry basket, and photos of the coating at the start and end.

Spec-sheet numbers stay in the "Manufacturer spec" column. Nothing moves into "Our measurement" until it has been measured.

## Cooking results

The fries test uses russets cut to about 3/8 inch, soaked, dried, tossed with a teaspoon of oil, in one even layer. {/* MEASURE: fries batch weight g, minutes to golden at 400°F, % of pieces evenly browned */} The full method is in the [Ninja AF101 french fries recipe](/recipes/air-fryer/ninja-af101-french-fries/).

The wings test uses flats and drums, patted dry, no sauce, one layer. Chef Approved Tools reports about five whole wings per batch at 400°F for 20 minutes, flipped halfway ([Chef Approved Tools](https://www.chefapprovedtools.com/reviews/ninja-air-fryer-af101)). {/* MEASURE: wing count, minutes to 175°F internal at 400°F, skin crispness notes */}

## What owners report

- **Coating wear**, mainly at the rim and after dishwasher use. Other owners say the AF101 coating outlasts cheaper baskets ([RedditRecs](https://redditrecs.com/air-fryer/model/ninja-af101/)).
- **Grease on the heating element** that is hard to reach.
- **Noise and cooking smell**, the top two complaints in Best Buy owner reviews; one owner said it "did smoke a bit." {/* MEASURE: visible smoke on wings or bacon, yes/no */}
- **Recalls.** I found no CPSC recall for the AF101 when I searched on Sep 26, 2026 ([CPSC recalls](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls)).

## Before you buy

- **AF100 or AF101.** Same 1550 W base and 4-quart basket; the AF101 adds a multi-layer rack. Buy whichever is cheaper if you will not use the rack.
- **Replacement baskets.** Third-party 4-quart baskets listed for the AF100 and AF101 are easy to find, which matters if the coating goes. {/* MEASURE: price and fit of a replacement basket, if the coating fails during the test */}
- **Presets.** Four functions only, with no bake or broil button. {/* MEASURE: roast mode result on a tray of vegetables, minutes at 390°F */}
