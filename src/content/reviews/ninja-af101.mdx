The Ninja AF101 is the small air fryer to get if you cook for one or two. TechGearLab made it its Editors' Choice and measured an average of 396.8°F at a 400°F setting. Owners rate it 4.8 of 5 across 4,081 Best Buy reviews. The catches: Consumer Reports measured only 2.6 usable quarts, and Ninja appears to be phasing it out.

## Is the AF101 being discontinued?

There are signs. As of Sep 27, 2026, Ninja's old AF101 product link redirects to the SharkNinja home page rather than to a product. The current SharkNinja AF101 address sends you to an error page. Consumer Reports labels it a model that may have limited distribution. The Amazon listing is still live. I found no official discontinuation notice. Consumer Reports says it expects the AF101 to perform like the Ninja AF100, the model it lab-tested, so an AF100 at a good price is a reasonable swap.

## What independent tests found

**Heat is close to the dial.** TechGearLab measured an average of 396.8°F at a 400°F setting and scored temperature accuracy 9.3 of 10 ([TechGearLab](https://www.techgearlab.com/reviews/kitchen/air-fryer/ninja-air-fryer)). In its comparison, that sits between the Instant Vortex Plus at 401.5°F and the Ninja Foodi 2-basket at 386.4°F ([TechGearLab best air fryers](https://www.techgearlab.com/topics/kitchen/best-air-fryer)). The AF101 earned the top overall score of 80 and the Editors' Choice.

**It's quick.** TechGearLab and Chef Approved Tools both report a 3-minute preheat, and TechGearLab scored cooking speed 9.4 of 10 ([Chef Approved Tools](https://www.chefapprovedtools.com/reviews/ninja-air-fryer-af101)). Food Network's Best Mid-Size pick, listed as the Ninja 4-Quart Air Fryer, browned everything it cooked well ([Food Network](https://www.foodnetwork.com/how-to/packages/shopping/product-reviews/best-air-fryer)).

**The controls are basic.** TechGearLab found the temperature moves in irregular 5 to 15°F steps. It also found no auto-pause when you pull the basket, no shake reminder, and a crisper plate whose grooves and holes make cleaning slower.

**It gets warm outside.** Chef Approved Tools says the basket handle gets hot enough to need an oven mitt. TechGearLab's figures for the hottest outside surface don't agree with each other: 146°F in the review text and 160°F in its comparison table. Both appear on TechGearLab's own pages (confirmed Sep 27, 2026), so treat the handle and body as hot to the touch either way.

## How much it holds

Four quarts is the basket volume, not what fits in one crisping layer. Consumer Reports measured 2.6 quarts of usable capacity. Chef Approved Tools fits about five whole wings per batch at 400°F for 20 minutes, flipped halfway. Reddit commenters summarized by RedditRecs call it right for one or two people ([RedditRecs](https://redditrecs.com/air-fryer/model/ninja-af101/)), and Food Network's con is the same: not for large households.

## What owners report

- **Very satisfied overall.** 4.8 of 5 at Best Buy across 4,081 reviews, and 98% would recommend it.
- **Noise** is the top complaint at Best Buy, with 55 mentions, usually as "a little loud." Chef Approved Tools calls the fan audible but not loud. Nobody I found has published a decibel figure.
- **Smell and smoke** come next, with 32 mentions; some owners say a little water in the drawer helps.
- **Temperature control** gets 11 mentions, matching TechGearLab's point about coarse steps.
- **Coating.** RedditRecs' summary has owners saying the ceramic coating lasts better than cheaper baskets. Chef Approved Tools warns that metal utensils scratch it permanently, so use wood, silicone or plastic. None of the sources I read show a pattern of coatings peeling. For grease on the element and basket care, see [how to clean the Ninja AF101](/guides/how-to-clean-ninja-af101/).

## Recalls

I found no CPSC recall for the AF101 as of Sep 27, 2026 ([CPSC](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls)).

## Who should buy it, and who should skip it

**Buy it** if you cook for one or two, want something fast and accurate, and have little counter space. Near $120, it has the strongest lab record of the small baskets we cover.

**Skip it** if you cook for three or more. The 2.6-quart measured capacity means batches. The [Instant Vortex Plus 6 quart](/reviews/instant-vortex-plus-6qt/) has 87.8 square inches of cooking area to this one's 53.5. If you need two foods at once, look at the [Ninja DZ201](/reviews/ninja-dz201/). Between this and Ninja's 5-quart Air Fryer Pro, see [Ninja AF101 vs AF141](/vs/ninja-af101-vs-ninja-af141/).
