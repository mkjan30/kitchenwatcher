The Ninja AF141 (Air Fryer Pro 4-in-1) is a 5-quart, 1,750 W basket air fryer that tops out at 400°F, listed at $129.99 on Sep 26, 2026. {/* MEASURE: verdict — one sentence, who it's for, and the number that decided it (e.g. grams of fries per layer or preheat seconds) */} It's built for one to three people who want air fry, roast, reheat and dehydrate, and nothing else.

The question that matters on this model is how much of that 5 quarts is usable. Consumer Reports lists it as 5 qt claimed and 3.8 qt measured ([Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org/appliances/air-fryers/ninja-pro-af141/m413991/)). My version of that number is grams of fries in one layer. The method for every test is on [how we test](/how-we-test/). {/* MEASURE: one line on test period, days used and number of cooks */}

## Specs, price and availability

Per SharkNinja's product page (manufacturer spec):

- **Capacity:** 5 quarts; "holds up to 4 lbs fries or 5 lbs chicken wings"
- **Power:** 1,750 W, 120 V, 14.6 A
- **Max temperature:** 400°F
- **Functions:** Air Fry, Roast, Reheat, Dehydrate
- **Size and weight:** 14.84 x 11.34 x 10.55 in, 10.67 lb
- **Cord:** 31.2 in
- **Warranty:** 1 year

**Availability:** the AF141 was out of stock on SharkNinja's own site on Sep 26, 2026, with a restock sign-up in place of the buy button. It was still listed at other retailers, including Abt at about $129.99 the same day. {/* MEASURE: price paid, retailer and in-stock status on purchase day */}

Dishwasher safety is unclear from the listings. Amazon's title calls the basket dishwasher-safe; Abt's listing says the nonstick parts are hand-wash only. {/* MEASURE: what the manual in the box says about dishwasher use, quote the line */}
