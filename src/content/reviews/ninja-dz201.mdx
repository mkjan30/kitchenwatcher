The Ninja DZ201 is an 8-quart air fryer split into two independent 4-quart baskets, so you can cook a main and a side at different settings and have them finish together. {/* MEASURE: one-sentence verdict from our results — basket-to-basket gap in °F and minutes, noise dB, Smart Finish accuracy — and who it suits */} One thing to know first: Ninja's own site now sends DZ201 shoppers to the newer 10-quart DZ901.

{/* MEASURE: photo — unit on counter with ruler for scale, both baskets closed */}

## Is the DZ201 being discontinued?

As of Sep 26, 2026, the DZ201 product page on Ninja's site redirects to the DZ901, a 10-quart model with two 5-quart baskets listed at $249.99. The DZ901 page does not mention the DZ201, and I found no official discontinuation notice. The DZ201 is still sold elsewhere: Target listed it in stock at $199.99 on Sep 26, 2026, and Consumer Reports shows it from $187.99 ([Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org/appliances/air-fryers/ninja-foodi-2-basket-dz201/m401887/)). Ninja still hosts the DZ201 owner's guide. My read: Ninja no longer sells it direct, and retail stock will thin out.

## Specs and price

Per Target's listing: 1690 W, two 4-quart baskets, 105 to 450°F across functions, 12.4 by 13.86 by 15.63 inches, 17.86 lb, and six functions (air fry, air broil, roast, bake, reheat, dehydrate). Baskets and crisper plates are dishwasher safe. Smart Finish staggers the start times so both baskets end together; Match Cook copies one basket's settings to the other.

## How this review is tested

Every air fryer on this site goes through the same run so the numbers line up across reviews. For the DZ201, every test runs in both baskets at once, then in one basket alone, because that is where owners say it falls short ([full method](/how-we-test/)):

- **Heat:** a probe thermometer clipped at basket center logs the climb from a cold start to 400°F, then the average swing around the set point for 10 minutes. The display saying "ready" does not count.
- **Food:** one even layer of fresh-cut fries and one layer of wings, weighed and counted, cooked at 400°F until golden or 175°F internal. Browning is scored piece by piece, not by eye across the pile.
- **Noise:** a sound meter 1 m away at basket height, with the kitchen's background level noted so it can be subtracted.
- **Power and heat:** a plug-in watt meter for the whole cycle, and an IR thermometer on the housing, handle and window after 20 minutes at 400°F.
- **Cleanup:** a stopwatch from last bite to dry basket, and photos of the coating at the start and end.

## The two-basket question

The point of this machine is two zones, and the most common owner complaint in our product notes is that the two baskets cook differently on identical settings. The test: the same weight of fries in each basket, same setting, started together, pulled when each is golden. {/* MEASURE: minutes to golden, left vs right; % evenly browned each side; °F deviation each side */}

Smart Finish gets its own test: salmon in one basket, broccoli or fries in the other, with different times. {/* MEASURE: seconds apart at finish; internal temps of both foods at the end */} The [Ninja dual zone salmon and broccoli recipe](/recipes/air-fryer/ninja-dual-zone-salmon-and-broccoli/) uses this exact setup.

## DZ201 or DZ901?

If the DZ201 disappears from shelves, the DZ901 is what Ninja offers in its place. On paper the DZ901 has two 5-quart baskets instead of two 4-quart ones and swaps air broil for a Max Crisp setting, at $249.99 on Ninja's site on Sep 26, 2026. {/* MEASURE: if we buy a DZ901 later, the same basket-gap test; until then, whether the DZ201's per-basket capacity is enough for 4 servings */} The DZ201 makes sense only while it sells for meaningfully less.
