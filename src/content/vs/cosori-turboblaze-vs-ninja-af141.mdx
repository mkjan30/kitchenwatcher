The Ninja AF141 is 5 quarts, 1750 W and tops out at 400°F, per SharkNinja's. ## Speed and temperature

COSORI's own claim is that the TurboBlaze cooks up to 46% faster than its older CP168 on a pound of fresh fries, per its launch press release. That's a claim against COSORI's own older model, not against Ninja. TechGearLab measured an average of 391.6°F at a 400°F setting on the TurboBlaze ([TechGearLab](https://www.techgearlab.com/topics/kitchen/best-air-fryer)) and hadn't tested the AF141.
