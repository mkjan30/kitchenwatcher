In the Hamilton Beach vs Cuckoo rice cooker choice, buy the Cuckoo CR-0655F if you want custom soak and heat settings and care most about rice quality. CNN Underscored ranked it above the Hamilton Beach, and Engadget named it best for most people. Buy the Hamilton Beach 37548 if you want half the price, up to 14 cups cooked and dishwasher-safe parts. It's Serious Eats' top pick.

The testers don't agree, though. America's Test Kitchen rated the Cuckoo only Recommended with Reservations, and Reviewed found the Hamilton Beach overcooked the bottom of a 1-cup batch. Nothing here was tested first-hand; every finding below is credited to the outlet that reported it.

## The short verdict: big and simple, or small and programmable

- **Hamilton Beach 37548 ($67 at Amazon on Nov 18, 2026):** for big batches (up to 7 rice cups), hot cereal and steamed vegetables, and anyone who wants the pot in the dishwasher.
- **Cuckoo CR-0655F ($140 at Amazon on Nov 18, 2026):** for people who want custom soak and heat settings, GABA and brown-rice presets, and an Auto Clean steam cycle, and who don't mind hand washing the pot.

Four outlets reviewed the Cuckoo CR-0655F (CNN Underscored, Serious Eats, ATK, Engadget) and three the Hamilton Beach (Serious Eats, CNN Underscored, Reviewed). Serious Eats and CNN Underscored covered both, and CNN compared them directly. Engadget's "best budget" Hamilton Beach is a different model. It links to another Amazon listing and is described as an 8-cup, under-$50 cooker, so its findings aren't used for the 37548 here.

## Which makes better rice? Serious Eats, CNN, Reviewed and ATK disagree

[Serious Eats](https://www.seriouseats.com/the-best-rice-cookers) names the Hamilton Beach its best rice cooker after testing 26 and has recommended it since 2019. It says the cooker made the fluffiest, best-textured rice in the shortest time and gave good results even with small measuring errors, but large batches are more prone to drying out or browning.

[CNN Underscored](https://www.cnn.com/cnn-underscored/reviews/best-rice-cooker) says the Hamilton Beach "almost made our winners' circle", but the Cuckoo edged it out: the Hamilton Beach couldn't quite match the Cuckoo's quality. CNN found the Cuckoo's rice a bit stickier and gummier than the Zojirushi Neuro Fuzzy's, and says it performed similarly to a Tiger that costs more than twice as much.

[Reviewed](https://www.reviewed.com/cooking/best-right-now/the-best-rice-cookers) is the dissent on the Hamilton Beach. It lists the cooker under other models it tried, says it had trouble cooking one cup without overcooking the bottom in some areas, and calls the rice "okay, but not stellar", with a tendency to stick to the pot.

On the Cuckoo, [ATK](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/equipment-reviews/2328-rice-cookers) got great long-grain white rice, but short-grain white and brown came out "a little wet" at Cuckoo's recommended ratios, and it rated the cooker Recommended with Reservations. [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/home/kitchen-tech/best-rice-cooker-120015478.html) says it did a particularly good job with long-grain basmati. Its tester preferred the white rice after half an hour on warm, and the brown rice could have cooked a touch longer.

No outlet rated either cooker bad. The complaints are specific: on the Hamilton Beach, Reviewed's is a 1-cup batch and Serious Eats' is large batches; on the Cuckoo, ATK's is short-grain and brown rice at the printed water lines.

## Capacity: the 14-cup and 6-cup labels measure different things

Hamilton Beach's guide rates the 37548 at 2 to 14 cups of cooked rice, with a maximum of 7 rice cups filled to the 7 line. Its cup holds 6 oz (177 ml), about 3/4 of a US cup. Cuckoo's manual rates the CR-0655F by raw rice: white 2 to 6 cups, mixed, brown and GABA 2 to 4, porridge 1 to 1.5.

So the Hamilton Beach's 14 cups and the Cuckoo's 6 aren't on the same scale. A single cup is where Reviewed saw the Hamilton Beach overcook the bottom. The [rice cooker cup size guide](/guides/rice-cooker-cup-size/) converts the cups, and [what size rice cooker do I need](/guides/what-size-rice-cooker-do-i-need/) matches capacity to household size.

## Settings: six presets vs micom with My Mode

The Hamilton Beach guide lists White Rice, Quick Rice (which skips the soak), Whole Grain, Hot Cereals, Steam Cook and Heat/Simmer, plus delay start up to 15 hours. Reviewed says its steamer basket doubles as a colander for rinsing rice.

The Cuckoo is a micom cooker, and its manual lists My Mode (longer soak and longer heat, each in 4 steps), GABA, Turbo, Auto Clean steam cleaning and a preset of up to 13 hours. Engadget notes you can program exact soak and heat times or stick with the presets.

Serious Eats says beginners may find the Cuckoo's presets and customization options overwhelming. [Induction vs micom rice cookers](/guides/induction-vs-micom-rice-cooker/) explains what micom means.

## Keep warm: 12-hour auto-off vs a 12-hour recommendation

Hamilton Beach's guide says WARM counts up to 12 hours, then the cooker turns off; it also suggests resting rice on WARM at least 10 to 15 minutes for softer results. Cuckoo's manual recommends keeping rice warm for less than 12 hours to avoid discoloring or odor. It says mixed and brown rice keep warm worse than plain rice and shouldn't be kept warm if at all possible; the elapsed-time display blinks at 24 hours.

Neither manual gives a food-safety time limit. [FoodSafety.gov](https://www.foodsafety.gov/keep-food-safe/4-steps-to-food-safety) says to keep cooked food at 140°F or above if it isn't served right away, and its [temperature chart](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures) says to reheat leftovers to 165°F. [How long rice can stay in a rice cooker](/guides/how-long-can-rice-stay-in-rice-cooker/) covers the Bacillus cereus risk.

## Cleaning and build complaints for each cooker

Hamilton Beach's guide shows the pot, steamer basket, paddle and measuring cup as dishwasher-safe, but not on the SANI cycle, and the lid liner washed by hand. Serious Eats editor Grace Kelly, who owns the cooker, says the hinged lid is prone to falling down when lifted. Reviewed calls it bulky.

Cuckoo's manual says to wash the inner pot with neutral detergent and not to use tough or metal scrubbers or sharp tools, or the coating may peel. ATK found the water lines, imprinted in the metal, hard to read, and the buttons hard to decipher. CNN Underscored says the Auto Clean steam cycle takes 25 minutes.

Engadget says the recessed water lines need a closer eye to keep starch from getting trapped, and the lid springs open hard enough to rock the unit. CNN Underscored notes there's no handle.

## Is the Cuckoo worth the extra $73?

On Nov 18, 2026, Reviewed's price listing showed the Hamilton Beach at $66.73 at Amazon and Walmart, $66.95 at Best Buy and $66.99 at Target; Serious Eats showed $67 at Amazon. ATK and Serious Eats showed the Cuckoo at $140 at Amazon the same day. Per the two manuals, the extra money buys micom control, My Mode, GABA and an Auto Clean cycle. It doesn't buy capacity or dishwasher-safe parts.
