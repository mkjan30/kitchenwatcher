The Instant Pot Rio vs Duo Plus choice comes down to a few extras. Per Instant, the Duo Plus adds low pressure (its Egg program defaults to Low), Sous Vide, Cake and Sterilize programs, a WhisperQuiet steam release and two extra sealing rings. The Rio pressure-cooks at high pressure only. Both are 1000 W, 6-quart cookers, per Instant's manuals.

Buy the Rio if you mainly pressure-cook, make rice and steam, and it's cheaper on the day. If you want those extras, the Duo Plus ($139.99 at Instant on Nov 13, 2026) is the better-equipped cooker.

Nobody at Kitchen Watcher has used either cooker. This page compares Instant's two current US manuals with what Wirecutter, America's Test Kitchen, TechGearLab and Reviewed found. [How we research](/how-we-research/) explains the method.

## What the Duo Plus adds: low pressure, Sous Vide, Sterilize and a quieter release

Low pressure is the biggest functional difference. The [Duo Plus V4 manual](https://p.widencdn.net/irxr19/112-0169-01_113-0058-01_IB_DuoPlusV4_US_Manual_R24-FINAL-06-30-2025_LoRes.pdf) lists default pressure levels for each Pressure Cook program: Custom, Chicken, Pasta, Soup, Beef, Beans and Sterilize run at High, and Egg runs at Low. Every pressure program in the [Rio 6-qt manual](https://p.widencdn.net/zaiedj/112-0312-01_112-0320-01_112-0321-01_112-0322-01_112-2213-01_112-0041-01_RIO6QT_US_Manual_399-1255-03US_R37_FINAL_10-19-2026_LoRes.pdf) is High. The [Rio Wide manual](https://media-www.canadiantire.ca/manual/product/0439809/0439809-1-76932d79-d779-4ab7-9028-c556b46ae86c.pdf), for a 7.5-quart sibling, states it plainly: "1 pressure level".

Instant's own program table only uses Low for eggs. [Wirecutter](https://www.nytimes.com/wirecutter/reviews/best-electric-pressure-cooker/) makes the same point from the other side: most pressure cooker recipes are written for high pressure. So if your cooking is stews, beans, rice and chicken, the Rio's single level covers the same defaults. Low pressure matters if you follow recipes written for it.

The Duo Plus also adds three programs. Sous Vide cooks food in an airtight bag underwater, with a maximum of 99 hours 30 minutes on most presets (24 hours on Egg). Cake uses pressurized steam, "especially cheesecakes". Sterilize defaults to 10 minutes at High and runs up to 30, per its manual.

The release mechanism differs too. Instant describes the Duo Plus release as WhisperQuiet; the Rio uses an Easy-Release switch. Instant's Duo Plus page also includes two extra sealing rings, which the Rio page doesn't mention.

## What the Rio and Duo Plus share, per Instant's manuals

On paper, the two cookers are closer than their names suggest. Both manuals list 1000 W and 6 quarts (5.7 L). Both require at least 1½ cups (375 mL) of liquid to pressure-cook, and both say not to fill past the PC MAX line.

Delay Start runs from 10 minutes to 24 hours on both. Both manuals say to replace the sealing ring every 12 to 18 months. Both use a Seal/Vent release, and the Rio manual warns that natural release can take "up to 40 minutes or more".

Instant's product pages describe the same 18/8 stainless pot with a tri-ply bottom and "over 10" safety mechanisms for both. Both pages say the lid and inner pot are dishwasher safe.

## What Wirecutter, ATK, TechGearLab and Reviewed found

[Wirecutter](https://www.nytimes.com/wirecutter/reviews/best-electric-pressure-cooker/) (updated Feb 24, 2026) is the one source we read that tested both. It makes the Rio its top pick and calls it "the latest evolution of the Instant Pot Duo". It says the Duo Plus adds only sous vide, cake and sterilize, plus low pressure, for about $30 more at the time of publication, and recommends it only on sale at a price close to the Rio's.

In its timing of a pot of beans, the Rio reached pressure in 14 minutes 35 seconds and the Duo Plus in 16 minutes 8 seconds; Wirecutter called the spread across four Instant Pots not significant. Wirecutter doesn't say which Duo Plus version it tested.

[America's Test Kitchen](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/equipment_reviews/2242-multicookers) (May 14, 2025) rated the Rio 6-quart Recommended with Reservations. It made decent rice, pressure-cooked respectably, and beans and beef stew were good with a few extra minutes. But ATK found it "didn't slow-cook well", its sauté "seemed underpowered", the pot has no handles and the interface is crowded.

[TechGearLab](https://www.techgearlab.com/topics/kitchen/best-pressure-cooker) (updated Nov 24, 2024) named the Duo Plus V4 its Best Overall with an overall score of 86, the top score it lists. It praised a "quieter, safer" steam valve whose lever sits on the other side of the lid from where steam is released. Its complaints: the pot is hard to scrub after sautéing, sauté is "finicky" and worked best on low, and the cooker is too large for some shelves.

[Reviewed](https://reviewed.com/cooking/best-right-now/the-best-electric-multi-cookers-and-instant-pots) (updated Nov 12, 2025) named the Duo Plus its Best Pressure Cooker for Most People and says its steam release is "as quiet as advertised". Reviewed says it tested a previous version of the model and doesn't name the version. Its spec box says 8 quarts while its text says 6, so treat its sizing with care.

ATK's well-reviewed Duo Evo Plus is listed under its discontinued models and is a different cooker, so its slow-cooking praise doesn't carry over to the Duo Plus V4.

## Instant Pot Rio review in brief

Instant's Rio page lists 13 one-touch Smart Programs. The manual's control panel shows Pressure Cook, Soup/Broth, Bean/Chili, Meat/Stew, Poultry, Rice, Multigrain, Egg, Steam, Slow Cook, Sauté, Yogurt and Keep Warm. Sauté and Slow Cook offer Low, Medium and High temperature levels.

Wirecutter and ATK are the two independent tests of this exact model we could read, and they lean different ways. Wirecutter rates it the best electric pressure cooker. ATK found it fine for pressure cooking and rice but weak for slow cooking and browning. Taken together, it suits someone who wants a straightforward pressure cooker and won't rely on it as a slow cooker.

## Where Instant's manuals and the testers disagree

The initial test run is the clearest conflict. The Rio 6-qt manual says "Use 3 quarts water, but no food." The Duo Plus V4 manual says "Add 3 cups water to the cooking pot." We haven't resolved which is right; follow your own manual and see the [Instant Pot water test guide](/guides/instant-pot-water-test/).

The Duo Plus program count is muddled. Instant's page lists 9 cooking functions in text (including steam and cake), an image that names Eggs and Sterilize instead, and 25 recipe presets. Reviewed repeats the 9 functions and 25 presets.

Prices vary by source and retailer: on Nov 13, 2026 the Rio showed at $90 to $122 and the Duo Plus at $139.99 to $191.19 in the listings above. Slow cooking is also uneven evidence: ATK faulted the Rio, and the sources we read say nothing comparable about the Duo Plus V4.

## Safety and recall status of the Rio and Duo Plus

Neither model appears in a CPSC recall in our search of the recall database on Nov 13, 2026. The only Instant Pot recall it returned is the [2015 Instant Pot Smart recall](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2015/Instant-Pot-Pressure-Cookers-Recalled-by-Double-Insight) for a shock hazard, a different model. Both manuals say not to fill past PC MAX, not to force the lid off and not to open it until all pressure is released; the Rio manual adds that the float valve drops when it has.

For what a lid-opening recall looks like in another brand, see SharkNinja's [Ninja Foodi OP300 recall (CPSC 25-247)](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2025/SharkNinja-Recalls-1-8-Million-Foodi-Multi-Function-Pressure-Cookers-Due-to-Burn-Hazard-Serious-Burn-Injuries-Reported) and [are pressure cookers safe](/guides/are-pressure-cookers-safe/), and check any cooker with the [pressure cooker recall check](/guides/pressure-cooker-recall-check/).

## Which to buy: Rio for basics, Duo Plus for low pressure and extras

- **Buy the Rio** if you mainly pressure-cook, make rice and steam. It's Wirecutter's top pick and was the cheaper of the two in every listing we saw on Nov 13, 2026. Don't buy it as a slow cooker, per ATK.
- **Buy the Duo Plus** if you want low pressure, Sous Vide, Sterilize or Cake, or the extra sealing rings. TechGearLab and Reviewed made it their top pick.
- **Wait for a sale** on the Duo Plus if you only want it for one extra. Wirecutter recommends it only when it's priced near the Rio.
