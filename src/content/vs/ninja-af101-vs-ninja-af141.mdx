## The short version

- **Cooking for one or two, short on counter depth:** the AF101 is 13.3 inches tall and 13.6 inches deep, per Target's listing.
- **Cooking for two to four, or cooking wings in one go:** the AF141's extra quart matters. How much more food fits in one layer: {/* MEASURE: grams of fries and wing count per single layer, both */}.
- **Want it to last:** see the cleanup and coating results below and [how to clean the Ninja AF101](/guides/how-to-clean-ninja-af101/).

## Speed

On paper the AF141 has 200 more watts. Several comparison sites say it crisps frozen food about 25% faster, but I couldn't find that claim on a Ninja page, so I'm testing it: {/* MEASURE: frozen fries minutes, both, and the percentage difference */}.
